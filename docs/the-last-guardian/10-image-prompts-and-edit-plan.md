# PART B — FINAL EDITING PLAN

## B.1 Post-production pipeline

```
1  SHOT GENERATION      → 306 shots from 09-veo-prompts.md, seeded per shot, 3 takes each
2  QC PASS              → Doc 08 Part C checklist on every take; rejects logged, re-rendered
3  SELECTS              → 1 hero take per shot; 2nd take retained as protection
4  ASSEMBLY             → Scene-by-scene, in script order (never out of order — the film's
                          emotional escalation is built on adjacency)
5  ROUGH CUT            → target 2h 22m
6  FRAME-MATCH LOCK     → Sc. 1 ↔ Sc. 15 conformed frame-for-frame before anything else is cut
7  PICTURE REFINE       → 3 passes: structure → performance → rhythm
8  VFX FINISHING        → hero-effect polish, interactive light, continuity paint-outs
9  SOUND                → dialogue edit → foley → ambience → the 38 Hz spine → premix
10 SCORE                → recorded to picture lock; 65% of the film left unscored
11 FINAL MIX            → Dolby Atmos; the Sc. 29K silence calibrated in a real cinema
12 GRADE                → three-palette LUT structure (Delta / Flats / Ark)
13 CONFORM & DELIVER    → 2.39:1, DCP + HDR master, target 2h 09m
```

## B.3 The ten cuts the film lives or dies on

| # | Cut | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **1J → black → title cards** | Cut 4 frames BEFORE his hand touches the lever. Hold black + total silence for exactly 3 seconds. If the audience shifts in their seats, it's right |
| 2 | **6I hero bridge** | Do not cut into the bastion's formation. One unbroken 9-second crane. The temptation to cover it in six angles must be refused |
| 3 | **11A first Flats frame** | Cut from the roar of Sc. 10's sluice gate directly to silence. **No audio pre-lap, no crossfade.** The drop from −28 dB to −72 dB is the cut |
| 4 | **15D the continuation** | The audience must recognise the shot before they realise it isn't stopping this time. Play the first 5 seconds at exactly the Sc. 1 rhythm, then let it run 3 seconds long before Dev's line |
| 5 | **15H the eleven seconds** | Hold on four faces for a full eleven seconds with no cut and no score. This is the longest static shot in the film and it is the reason the third act works |
| 6 | **17A the sunlight** | Cut from grey rain to hot sunlight on a single frame, no dissolve. Colour returning should feel like an assault |
| 7 | **18N the useless wall** | Hold 12 seconds — 4 seconds longer than is comfortable. The audience must arrive at "it can't move" by themselves |
| 8 | **27L the city stopping** | 12 seconds, one slow lateral dolly, no cutaways, no score, no VFX. Do not intercut with the Ark. The single most disciplined cut in the film |
| 9 | **29K the 38 Hz drop** | The hum that has been running since frame one stops. Give it 4 seconds of nothing before Kira drags him out. Cinemas will feel it physically |
| 10 | **30O final hold** | Hold the kite for 18 seconds. Cut to black on a rising note, not a resolved one. Title card in silence |

## B.4 Editorial rules
- **Never cut to a reaction shot during a Hollow scene.** Their scenes are played in masters; the absence of coverage is the discomfort.
- **The notebook inserts are always the same frame.** Locked-off, 40 mm macro, directly overhead. Seven appearances. Any variation reads as a mistake.
- **No slow motion anywhere in the film** except two uses, both under 1 second: the rivet in Sc. 6, and the tether snapping in Sc. 18M.
- **No intercutting during Scene 17.** The parley plays as an unbroken block. No cutaways to the real world.
- **Match-cut discipline:** three intentional match cuts only — 3C white kites → 30B coloured kites; 6M dissolving light-dust → 7B forty screens; 29J extinguishing memories → 29K spines going dark.
- **Trailer-proofing:** the film contains no line of dialogue that functions as a trailer beat. If a line sounds quotable out of context, rewrite it.

## B.7 Practical production notes for AI-assisted generation

1. **Generate in this order:** reference stills → location plates → Scene 1 → Scene 15 (using Scene 1's plates) → everything else in script order.
2. **Seeds:** lock one seed per character per costume state and reuse it. Record it in `data/qc-log.csv`.
3. **Chaining:** for continuous action within a scene, use the final frame of shot *n* as the first-frame reference for shot *n+1*.
4. **Never regenerate an approved reference still.** If the model drifts, re-condition from the approved still rather than re-prompting from text.
5. **Three takes minimum** on any shot containing two or more principals — multi-character identity drift is the most common failure mode.
6. **Audio is generated separately.** Do not rely on video-model audio for anything except temp. The 38 Hz spine, the chimes and the silences must be built in the sound department.
7. **Budget the silences.** Roughly 11 minutes of this film has no music and near-zero ambience. That is the most expensive thing in it, and the cheapest to render.
