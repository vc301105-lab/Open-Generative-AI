### A.2 The Cradle
Not alien, not magical, not divine — the film never explains it and must never be tempted to. What is known on screen:
- A self-organising crystalline lattice ~1.1 km across, 400 m beneath the delta silt, predating the coastline by an unknown margin.
- It carries and stores **resonance** — a standing waveform that human myelin can conduct if threaded.
- It **records**. Every Threadwork carrier who dies within range leaves a readable trace. This is why Dev spent four years drowning in other people's deaths, and why the anchor-stone can hold a person's memory.
- It is dormant unless drawn upon. It does not want anything. Do not anthropomorphise it in dialogue.

### A.3 The Guardian Corps (dissolved)
- **Structure:** Bastion (structural), Tether (relational), Current (kinetic), Veil (perceptual), Loom (systemic — Dev alone). Only two classes survive into the present: Arjun (Bastion) and Kira (Tether).
- **Creed, spoken twice in the film (Sc. 11 by Arjun, Sc. 30 by Kira):**
  > *"Anchor before you build. Never shield what you can move. The current takes payment in you."*
- **Iconography:** a hexagon with one open side — a wall that admits it isn't finished. Appears on gorgets, the Drowned Cathedral floor, the Ark's hull, and finally as Kira's new mark.

### A.4 The Coastal Authority
Civil-military successor to the Corps. Storm-grey and oxblood. Motto: *"Hold the line, count the cost."* Ostensibly the delta's disaster service; in practice a carrier-control apparatus. Not villainous — **frightened at scale**. Ordinary officers in this film help people, evacuate towers, and also fire Nullpins into crowds. Both, in the same scene, is the point.

### A.5 The Hollow
Dev's order. Between 300 and 400 members. Recruited from the Bleached, the bereaved and ex-Corps auxiliaries. **They are volunteers.** Their uniform is bone-white lacquer over grey weave — the only clean thing in a filthy city, which is precisely why the city fears them. They do not shout, they do not taunt, they move at a walking pace and they are unbelievably calm. Their harvests are conducted with paperwork.

### A.6 Society & texture
- **Boat people / Stiltway:** the majority of the delta's poor live in vertical stilt-towers linked by rope walkways and salvaged shipping containers. Rain is constant; everything is tarpaulin, LED string, plastic drum, marigold, corrugated steel.
- **Kite culture:** the delta's kids run kites off the top decks in the monsoon breaks. Glass-coated lines, cutting duels, gambling. Kira is a legend on the Stiltway's roofs. This is the film's only joy and it must be shot beautifully.
- **Carrier stigma:** "spark", "wire", "lit one" — street slang, roughly the register of an infectious disease. Carrier tattoos (a registered wrist barcode) are visible on background players.
- **Language:** English-primary with Tamil, Bengali and Yoruba code-switching in market and family scenes. Subtitle non-English lines; never subtitle the Hollow (they speak plainly, always).
- **Religion/ritual:** the Corps dead are remembered by hanging a paper kite from a walkway on 3 August. In Scene 3 the walkways are thick with them — it is the twelfth anniversary week. This is planted for Scene 30, where the sky is full of them again, in colour.

| **Bleaching** | Memory-consolidation damage from over-draw. Not amnesia in the movie sense — he doesn't forget how to speak or tie a knot; he loses *episodic* memory: names, faces, events, the last week. | Show it, never explain it twice. ### Technology design language
- **Corps/legacy tech:** brass, copper, leather, hand-numbered, repairable, warm. Analogue gauges. Everything looks like it was made by someone who expected to fix it themselves.
- **Authority tech:** stamped grey composite, oxblood stencils, riveted, institutional, slightly too heavy.
- **Hollow tech:** bone-white lacquer, seamless, no fasteners visible, no labels, no serial numbers, no wear. Nothing has a name on it.
- **Stiltway tech:** everything is three other things. Boat motors made of pump parts, LED strings, plastic drum floats, tarpaulin.

## PART C — LOCATION BIBLE

> **LOCATION CONTINUITY LAW:** Each location below has a LOCKED SHEET. Architecture, geography, weather, time-of-day, hero objects and palette do not change between visits unless a "permitted change" is explicitly listed. Every generated shot in a location must cite its location code (e.g. `LOC-03`).

---

### LOC-01 — THE CRADLE CORE (2059) *(Scenes 1, 15)*
- **Architecture:** A cylindrical containment shaft 60 m across, 200 m deep. Concentric brass containment rings on hydraulic arms. Crystal filament growing through the walls like frost through cracked concrete. Catwalks at four levels.
- **Geography:** 400 m beneath the delta.
- **Weather / time:** N/A (interior). Emergency state throughout.
- **Light:** Amber sodium work-lamps + spinning red alarm gyros + the growing white bloom from below. Practical-heavy, high contrast.
- **Hero objects:** the containment ring lever (brass, chest-height, red-painted grip); Dev's pinned position on the level-3 catwalk; a fallen medic's kit; Arjun's cadet helmet.
- **Palette:** amber / oxide red / cold white.
- **Permitted change:** none. Scenes 1 and 15 are the SAME location at the SAME instant, shot identically. Frame-for-frame match required.

### LOC-02 — THE DROWNED CATHEDRAL *(Scenes 2, 23)*
- **Architecture:** The submerged Old Kaveri central transit hall. 40 m barrel-vaulted ceiling, now 18 m underwater. Cast-iron columns, a collapsed departure board, a Corps hexagon inlaid in the terrazzo floor, silt-drifted benches, a broken skylight throwing one shaft of green light.
- **Geography:** Under the Old Kaveri ruins, 6 km offshore of the Stiltway. Access via a broken skylight from above.
- **Weather / time:** Underwater. Perpetual green-blue dusk. Visibility 8 m.
- **Light:** One vertical god-ray through the skylight; Arjun's amber head-lamp; particulate everywhere.
- **Hero objects:** the terrazzo Corps hexagon; the departure board (frozen at 14:12, 3 Aug 2059); a chained rack of rusted Corps mantles (paid off in Sc. 23 when they take three).
- **Palette:** viridian / teal / one amber lamp.
- **Permitted change:** Sc. 23 has three mantles missing from the rack and a fresh silt cloud. Otherwise identical.

### LOC-03 — THE STILTWAY *(Scenes 3, 6, 7, 18, 21, 30)* — the film's home base
- **Architecture:** Nine stilt-towers, 14–22 storeys, built from stacked shipping containers, bamboo scaffold and corrugated steel, linked by 40+ rope-and-plank walkways at every fourth level. Tarpaulin awnings in faded saffron, teal and oxblood. LED string lights on every walkway. Marigold garlands at doorways. Water 11 m below at the market level.
- **Geography:** North-east delta. Tower **Bhaskar's Yard** (boat repair, ground/water level) at the south; **Tower Six** is the tallest and is where Kira flies kites; the **Long Walk** is the 120 m main walkway between Towers Three and Six — this is the collapse in Sc. 6 and the crossing in Sc. 21.
- **Weather:** Monsoon. Rain in every Stiltway scene except Sc. 30 (see below). Wind 20–35 km/h, gusting.
- **Time of day:** Sc. 3 late afternoon (grey), Sc. 6 dusk, Sc. 7 night, Sc. 18 golden pre-storm break, Sc. 21 night storm, Sc. 30 morning, clear.
- **Light:** LED string practicals (2700 K), sodium floods, oil-drum fires, and the flat grey monsoon key.
- **Hero objects:** the memorial paper kites hung along the Long Walk (Sc. 3 — white; Sc. 30 — coloured); Bhaskar's crane arm; the red water-tank on Tower Six's roof; a hand-painted sign reading "SIX RUPEES — NO CREDIT — I MEAN IT".
- **Palette:** saffron / teal / rust / wet grey.
- **Permitted change:** **Sc. 6 destroys the Long Walk's mid-span (40 m).** From Sc. 7 onward it is a visible gap with hanging planks and a temporary rope bridge. In Sc. 21 the rope bridge is in use. In Sc. 30 it has been rebuilt in fresh untreated bamboo — lighter in colour than everything around it. This progression is mandatory.

### LOC-04 — WARD 12 / MEERA'S CLINIC *(Scenes 5, 9, 20)*
- **Architecture:** Three shipping containers welded into an L, on the fourth deck of Tower Three. Inside: a dental chair repurposed as a surgical seat, pegboard walls of brass instruments, a wall of index cards (patient memory records), an aquarium with no fish used as a coolant tank, a curtain of x-ray film.
- **Weather / time:** interior; rain audible on steel roof always.
- **Light:** Two surgical work-lamps (5600 K, harsh), one warm desk lamp, green glow from the aquarium.
- **Hero objects:** the index-card wall; the dented tea tumbler; the stabiliser workbench; a framed 2058 Corps class photograph (Dev, Arjun, Amara, Meera in it — paid off in Sc. 16).
- **Palette:** clinical white-green with warm tungsten pockets.
- **Permitted change:** **Sc. 9 burns it.** Sc. 20 is the same space gutted and black, rain coming through the roof, the index-card wall a smear of ash, the class photo warped but legible. Never restore it.

### LOC-05 — ANJARI SALT FLATS *(Scenes 11, 13, 15-partial)*
- **Architecture:** None standing. 90 km of bleached, cracked white salt plain where a coastal district used to be. Ghost-foundations of buildings visible as low rectangles. A leaning, half-melted transmission pylon at the horizon. Bleached car shells.
- **Geography:** North of the Tidewall, the blast footprint of the Silence.
- **Weather / time:** **NO WIND. NO RAIN. NO BIRDS.** Permanent flat white overcast, 3 stops brighter than anywhere else in the film. Sound floor is near-zero — this is the "silence" of the title, and audiences should notice the absence physically.
- **Light:** Enormous soft top-light, no direction, no shadows to speak of. Skin looks bloodless here.
- **Hero objects:** the leaning pylon; a child's bleached bicycle frame; the ghost-outline of the Anjari market square where Amara and Ila died (Arjun and Kira stand in it in Sc. 11 without knowing).
- **Palette:** white / bone / the faintest cyan in shadow. The only saturated colour permitted in this location is the characters' own power.
- **Permitted change:** none, ever. The flats do not change. That is the horror of them.

### LOC-06 — BASTION SPIRE (Coastal Authority HQ) *(Scenes 4-ext, 7, 14, 21)*
- **Architecture:** A 300 m brutalist storm-tower on the last piece of real bedrock, storm-grey board-formed concrete, oxblood stencilled numerals, external buttresses, a rooftop skiff pad. Interior: long corridors, low ceilings, oxblood floor stripe, everything lit from below eye-line.
- **Sub-locations:** **Archive Level B7** (a sealed cold-room of physical evidence drums and a single playback booth); **Ops Deck 18** (Riya's floor, glass, rain-streaked, 40 monitors); **Ose's office, Level 30** (ivory, quiet, one enormous window, a scale model of the Tidewall).
- **Weather / time:** rain on every window, always. Sc. 14 is 03:40 (night ops). Sc. 21 is storm night.
- **Light:** Fluorescent green-cyan institutional; Archive B7 is amber emergency lighting only; Ose's office is warm ivory.
- **Hero objects:** the evidence drum labelled **INQ-0803-59 · SEALED · MINISTERIAL**; the playback booth's brass helmet-footage reader; Ose's scale model.
- **Palette:** storm-grey / oxblood / cyan fluorescent.

### LOC-07 — THE TIDEWALL *(Scene 10)*
- **Architecture:** A 40 m sea-wall, 60 km long, concrete with rusted steel teeth, gun-slit sluice gates every 400 m, service catwalks, moss and barnacle line at 12 m.
- **Geography:** Separates the living delta from the bleached north coast.
- **Weather / time:** Dawn, driving rain, heavy swell.
- **Light:** Pre-dawn blue with sweeping sodium searchlights from the sluice towers.
- **Hero objects:** Sluice Gate 31 (half-open, jammed, the one they run); the barnacle line; a rusted sign: "NORTH OF THIS LINE — NO RECOVERY".

---

### LOC-08 — THE ARK *(Scenes 22, 24-ext, 25, 26, 27)*
- **Architecture:** A converted deep-sea drilling rig, 240 m across, standing on four legs 80 m above the sea. Decks re-clad in seamless bone-white lacquer. A central cathedral-like **Assembly Hall** with a 30 m ceiling and no decoration whatsoever. **The Moon Pool** at the centre — a 40 m circular opening down to black water and the Cradle beneath. Living quarters with identical white cots, no personal objects, no photographs, nothing on any wall.
- **Weather / time:** Category-five storm approach through Sc. 24–27; the Ark's interior is silent and windless.
- **Light:** Even, sourceless, shadowless white — the exact opposite of the film's grammar everywhere else. Deliberately uncomfortable.
- **Hero objects:** the Moon Pool; the Concord Array (144 white transmission spines on the upper deck); Dev's single chair facing the Moon Pool; the one non-white object on the entire rig — Amara's red thread bracelet, on a nail by Dev's chair.
- **Palette:** bone-white / pale grey / black water. **Zero warm tones except the bracelet.**

---

### LOC-09 — THE CRADLE LOOM *(Scenes 28, 29)*
- **Architecture:** A 200 m spherical natural cavity beneath the Ark, entirely lined in standing crystal filament like the inside of a geode. A suspended brass-and-crystal ring platform 30 m across at the centre, reached by a single catwalk from the moon-pool shaft. Filament "strings" run floor to ceiling all around like a harp the size of a stadium.
- **Weather / time:** N/A. A low sub-bass hum, 38 Hz, present in every frame.
- **Light:** Emissive — the walls are the light source. Cool white-blue, rising to blinding white during the Concord.
- **Hero objects:** the ring platform; the central spindle; the eight primary strings (which Kira is meant to weave); Arjun's grounding point (a bare crystal shelf).
- **Palette:** white-blue → pure white → after the ground, dead grey.

---

### LOC-10 — THE KITE-FIELD, TOWER SIX ROOF *(Scenes 3, 18, 30)*
- **Architecture:** A 30×20 m corrugated roof deck with the red water-tank, a forest of antenna masts, drying laundry lines, pigeon coops, a painted hopscotch grid.
- **Weather / time:** Sc. 3 — grey, rain, wind. **Sc. 18 — a monsoon break: 11 minutes of golden light, the only warm exterior light in Acts I–II.** Sc. 30 — clear morning.
- **Light:** Sc. 18 is the film's beauty shot: low golden backlight through rain-haze, kites like embers.
- **Hero objects:** the red water-tank (Kira's name is scratched into it — camera finds it in Sc. 30); the coops; Kira's kite (saffron-and-black diamond, hand-repaired with tape).
