## PART A — VFX BIBLE

### A.0 The governing principle
> **Every effect in this film is a physical event with a cost, not a decoration.**
> If a VFX shot could be described as "cool", it is wrong. If it can be described as *load-bearing*, *expensive*, *heavy*, *painful* or *quiet*, it is right. The audience must be able to look at any frame and tell (a) who is spending energy, (b) what it is costing them, and (c) which direction the force is going.

Three hard prohibitions:
1. **No sparks-and-lightning "energy" language.** Nothing crackles. Cradle current is geometric, glassy and calm.
2. **No fire from powers.** Fire only ever comes from ordinary sources (the clinic, oil drums, welding).
3. **No screen-space UI.** No HUDs, no floating readouts, no holographic interfaces. The most advanced technology in this world is a brass box that plays a memory.

### A.1 HERO EFFECT 01 — BASTION HARDLIGHT (Arjun)
| Property | Spec |
|---|---|
| Colour | Core `#B9F2FF`, edge `#2FB8D6`, strained core shifts to `#FFAE5C` amber |
| Geometry | Hexagonal tessellation, 380 mm cell pitch, cells inherit a 4 mm bevel |
| Growth | Crystallises **outward from an anchor point** at ~40 m/s; never fades in |
| Material | 62% transmissive, index of refraction 1.42, refracts rain and background legibly |
| Shadow | **Casts a real, coloured shadow.** This is non-negotiable — it proves the light is solid |
| Interaction | Rain drums on it, dust settles on it, blood smears on it, Nullpins embed in it |
| Load response | Cells nearest a load develop amber stress fractures within 0.5 s; fracture density = force |
| Death | Does not shatter. **Dissolves into drifting light-dust** that falls and is rained through |
| Absolute rule | **It never moves.** Static relative to its anchor surface. Never thrown, never flown |

**Key deliverables:** Sc. 6I (the 40 m Stiltway bridge), Sc. 25B/C (the staircase of light, 80 m of leg), Sc. 18N (the useless wall), Sc. 26I (the deck-plate inversion), Sc. 29G (the full-body ground).

### A.3 HERO EFFECT 03 — THREADWORK
Sub-dermal dendritic filament following real anatomical nerve paths.
| State | Look | Scenes |
|---|---|---|
| Dormant | Matte silver-grey (Arjun) / matte gold-bronze (Kira). **No emission whatsoever** | all |
| Active | Emissive in the character's signature colour, running root → extremity in 0.3 s | on power use |
| Overdrawn | Amber-orange with a 6 Hz flicker; skin around it reddens | Sc. 10, 18, 25 |
| Grounded | Full-body white-out, conduits rupturing | Sc. 29G only |
| **Dead** | **Matte dead pewter, permanently, no emission ever again** | Sc. 29L, 30 |

### A.4 HERO EFFECT 04 — THE BLOOM (the Silence)
The single most important effect in the film and the one most likely to be got wrong.

**What it is NOT:** an explosion, a shockwave, a fireball, a nuke, a dust ring, a mushroom cloud.
**What it IS:** a slow, glassy, non-turbulent white expansion that travels at ~90 m/s, is completely silent, and does not destroy matter — it *stops* it. Objects and people inside it go white and simply cease to be lit as themselves. No debris. No smoke. No burning. Afterwards there is bleached salt and nothing else.

Reference feeling: a photographic overexposure spreading through a real space, or frost forming across glass at 400× speed.
**Deliverables:** Sc. 1G, 15F, 28L (the end of Amara's trace).

### A.6 HERO EFFECT 06 — MIRROR REFLECTION (Sera)
- Mirror plates must be **true ray-traced returns of the actual set** — if the set changes, her plates change.
- Reflected hardlight returns along its **identical incoming vector**, inverted to silver-white, with a **one-frame delay**. The delay is the tell.
- Chrome sclera on both eyes: full mirror, no iris, correct environment reflection, correct wet meniscus at the lid line.
- **Copy-step:** exactly three semi-transparent silver afterimages, 6-frame delay each, each landing exactly one strike, dissolving from the feet upward.
- **Crack progression:** left gauntlet from Sc. 9I; chest plate from Sc. 26I. Her own face must be legible in the cracks in Sc. 26K (nine fragments).

---

### A.7 HERO EFFECT 07 — VOLUMETRIC MEMORY PROJECTION
Not a hologram. A **light-field with photographic grain**: shoulders-up, correct real-world lighting *from the recorded scene* (not from the room it's playing in), edges that dissolve into drifting particles, a slow vertical scan-drift of 1 px/s, and no interface elements of any kind.

Critically: **the projection carries its own room tone.** Dev's recording in Sc. 8 sounds like the Cradle. Arjun's in Sc. 27 sounds like Bhaskar's rainy yard, inside a silent white room. That acoustic mismatch does more emotional work than any visual.

---

### A.8 HERO EFFECT 08 — THE CONCORD
- **Launch (Sc. 27K):** a soft white annulus expanding outward at the speed of weather. No shockwave, no fire, no debris, no lens distortion. It looks *gentle*, and that is why it is terrifying.
- **Effect on people (Sc. 27L):** **ZERO VFX.** Performance only. Four hundred faces going smooth and calm and stopping mid-motion. The complete absence of any effect is the effect. Any glow, any eye flare, any shimmer ruins the scene and the film.
- **The half-woven field (Sc. 28N):** a vast, slow, unstable lattice overhead, made of eight million faint threads that never resolve into a pattern.
- **The ground (Sc. 29G/H):** a 40 km column of white light standing for two seconds, seen from orbit. No smoke, no debris, no crater. Then gone.

## PART B — SOUND DESIGN

### B.1 The sound thesis
> This is a film about memory, and memory is an acoustic phenomenon. **The antagonist's signature is silence.** Every step toward Dev's world is a step toward a quieter mix. The heroes' world is loud, wet, cluttered and full of other people's business.

### B.2 The four sonic worlds
| World | Floor | Reverb | Character |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Stiltway** | −28 dB, dense | Cluttered, short, lots of tin and tarpaulin | Rain on ten thousand different materials; radios; three languages; engines; children |
| **The Authority** | −40 dB | Long, hard concrete | HVAC hum, fluorescent ballast buzz, boots, sealed doors |
| **The Salt Flats** | **−72 dB, near-anechoic** | **NONE. Voices land dead** | Footsteps on salt crust are too loud. No wind, no birds, no score |
| **The Ark** | **−80 dB, fully anechoic** | **NONE. No room tone at all** | Bare feet on lacquer. Nothing else. Deeply uncomfortable in a cinema |

### B.3 Signature sounds (the film's sonic fingerprints)
1. **38 Hz** — the Cradle's fundamental. Present, sub-audible, in every frame of the film from Sc. 1 to Sc. 29K. **It drops out at 29K and its absence is the loudest event in the film.** Audiences will feel their chest release.
2. **The Bastion chime** — a bowed glass harmonic, C#, with a 4 s tail. Rises a semitone per stair in Sc. 25B.
3. **The Tether tone** — a wet finger on a wine glass. Pitch rises with load. Six of them out of tune = danger.
4. **The Nullpin swallow** — a descending metallic gulp, *not* a gunshot. Deliberately anti-heroic.
5. **The resonance blade** — 4 kHz whine that **stops dead on contact**, no follow-through.
6. **Hollow silence** — the sled, the boots, the doors: they make no sound where sound should be. Foley the *absence* by ducking the ambience 6 dB whenever a Hollow enters frame.
7. **Two sternum taps** — Dev's gesture in Sc. 13C. Returns as a musical figure in Sc. 30M.
8. **Rain on hardlight** — Sc. 6J. Rain drumming on solid light: recorded as rain on a tensioned glass sheet, pitched down 3 semitones.

### B.4 Dialogue & mix rules
- **Nobody with power ever shouts.** Arjun, Dev, Riya, Sera, Ose and Meera all speak at conversational level even during catastrophe. **Only civilians shout.** The two exceptions are earned: Meera's "THEY'RE NOT CARDS—" (Sc. 9H) and Kira's "ARJUN—" (Sc. 18M).
- **Arjun's dialogue is mixed 1.5 dB drier and closer than everyone else's** throughout — he is always slightly more present than the world around him, which subtly signals that he is holding on.
- **Non-English lines are never ducked or musically underscored.** They play at full level and are subtitled.
- **Sc. 19 mix event:** direct sound drops out entirely for 9 seconds, leaving heartbeat and a 6 kHz tone; a room-tone stutter matches the picture's frame-repeat.
- **Sc. 27L mix event:** the entire city's sound fades over 2 s to rain only. No music. No cue. Just eight million people stopping.

### B.5 Foley priorities
Wet canvas. Brass on brass. Bare feet on lacquer. Paper (the notebook is a character and needs its own foley pass — waterlogged pages sound nothing like dry ones). Rope under load. Salt crust. A prosthetic servo. A gold tooth clicking. A kite line.

## PART C — MUSIC DIRECTION

### C.1 Palette
A **chamber-scale orchestra deliberately too small for the images**: 18 strings, solo cello, prepared piano, bass clarinet, two horns. Plus:
- **Glass harmonica and bowed crotales** — the Cradle/hardlight family (they sit inside the SFX chime so score and sound blur).
- **Kanjira, mridangam and a low frame drum** — the delta's pulse, used only for the fleet and the city.
- **A solo female voice, wordless, never processed** — the Amara motif. Only four appearances in the entire film.
- **Analogue tape hiss and a detuned upright piano** — the Memory theme's texture. As the film progresses the piano goes *further* out of tune.

**Explicitly banned:** braams, synth risers, trailer drums, "epic" choir, gladiatorial percussion for the finale, and any cue that tells the audience to feel triumphant during a sacrifice.

### C.2 The five themes

| Theme | Instrumentation | Meaning | Appearances |
|---|---|---|---|
| **THE GUARDIAN** (two notes, rising a fourth, then falling a third — literally "an anchor and a corner") | Solo cello → full strings | Duty as a job, not a glory | Sc. 6L (first full), 23J (only unironic statement), 25C (with Fleet), 30C (light, new orchestration) |
| **MEMORY** | Detuned upright piano + tape hiss | Arjun's condition | 5, 9G, 19 (solo, broken), 29C (solo piano, unaccompanied), 30G |
| **AMARA** | Solo wordless female voice | What was lost | 18E, 22G, 28L, 29F — **four times only, never more** |
| **THE CONCORD** | Glass harmonica + bowed crotales, perfectly in tune, no vibrato | The seduction of peace | 17 (unaccompanied), 22, 27K |
| **THE FLEET** | Kanjira + mridangam + low frame drum + horns | Ordinary people | 21I, 24C, 25C, 30A |

### C.3 Score placement law
The film is **65% unscored**. Music is forbidden in:
- Every Salt Flats scene except the training montage's final swing (Sc. 11J) and the very end of Sc. 11.
- The whole of Scene 17 (the Concord parley) — gulls and wind only.
- Scene 15 from the footage's start to Arjun's "Don't learn anything from this."
- Scene 27L (the city going quiet).

### C.5 End credits
**No pop song.** A single 6-minute suite: Memory theme → Amara's voice → Guardian theme, ending on the two taps and tape hiss running out.
