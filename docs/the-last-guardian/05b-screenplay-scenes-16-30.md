### SCENE 16
**INT. BASTION SPIRE — ARCHIVE B7 / SERVICE STAIR — 04:10**

> **OBJECTIVE:** Get out of a sealed archive. Meera wants to confess before she loses the nerve.
> **CONFLICT:** Four officers in the corridor; a truth that will blow the team apart mid-escape.
> **OUTCOME:** They escape. Kira learns Dev Aranya is her father — from a recording, in a stairwell, while running.
> **VALUE SHIFT:** A team (+) → Three people who can't look at each other (−).

Red light. The corridor door cycles.

Riya puts her Nullpin sidearm on the floor and slides it away — she will not shoot her own people.

**RIYA**
Service stair. Left, left, down eleven. When you hit the pump room, the wall's cardboard, kick it.

**ARJUN**
And you?

**RIYA**
I'm going to be *extremely* insubordinate in a corridor for ninety seconds.

She goes. Impact sounds. Someone shouts her rank.

Arjun, Kira and Meera run the service stair. Meera can't keep up — one arm, a shoulder wound, sixty-one years old. On a half-landing she grabs Arjun's wrist with the brass hand and *stops* him.

**MEERA**
The class photograph. In the clinic. Did you look at it. In twelve years, did you ever once *look* at it?

**ARJUN**
Meera, we don't have—

**MEERA**
[BENGALI] *Listen to me, boy.*
(she pulls the framed photo — warped, half-burnt — from her coat. She has been carrying it since the fire.)
2058. Corps class. Me. You. Dev.
(her finger on the fourth figure)
Amara Okonkwo. Field medic. Dev's wife.

Kira, three steps above, stops dead. The surname lands on her like a dropped weight.

**KIRA**
...Say that name again.

**MEERA**
(to Kira, and this is the hardest thing she has ever done)
I pulled you out of the Anjari salt when you were five years old. You were under her. She was still warm and you would not let go of her collar, and I broke two of your fingers getting you out, and I am sorry, and I would do it again.
(beat)
I forged your papers. I put you in the Stiltway. I never told either of you, because he would have come for you, and *he* —
(at Arjun)
— he would have kept you, and then forgotten you, and it would have finished him.

**KIRA**
(very quiet, very fast)
My father is dead. My father was a fisherman and he is *dead* —

**MEERA**
Your father is on the north coast, child, and he is building something with your name on the plans.

Klaxons. Boots above them. Nobody moves for two full seconds.

Then Kira turns and runs down the stairs alone, and Arjun follows, and the film has changed shape.

### SCENE 17
**THE CONCORD — MIND-SPACE (LOC-05, AS IT WAS) — ETERNAL NOON**

> **OBJECTIVE:** Dev wants Arjun to understand, and to bring him Kira willingly. Arjun wants to know what the Concord is.
> **CONFLICT:** Dev is right about everything except the answer, and Arjun has just learned he is the reason Dev is right.
> **OUTCOME:** The villain's plan, fully stated. Arjun refuses. Dev is not angry, which is much worse.
> **VALUE SHIFT:** Fear of a monster (−) → Fear of a good man (−−).

Arjun sleeps in the bottom of a boat. And then—

**SUNLIGHT.**

The Anjari coast **as it was**: a market square, bicycles, mango sellers, laundry, a school wall painted with fish, wind, gulls, a blue sea. Colour floods the film for the first time since Scene 1. It is *unbearably* beautiful.

The camera does not move in this scene. Not once. Dead-centre, symmetrical, 40 mm, locked.

**DEV ARANYA (58)** sits on a low wall in a charcoal coat, barefoot, a broken gorget at his throat and a wedding band on a chain. The left side of his jaw and neck is translucent crystal with slow light beneath it. **[CRYSTALLISATION STAGE 2]**

He's peeling an orange.

**DEV**
Sit. It's the third of August. Two o'clock. The market's about to get busy.

Arjun does not sit.

**DEV (CONT'D)**
I built this out of what the Cradle recorded. Everything you're looking at is a real afternoon that really happened. That woman argued about okra for six minutes.
(he points, fond)
Six minutes.

**ARJUN**
You're alive.

**DEV**
I'm *retained.* It's not the same and I don't recommend it.
(he offers a segment of orange; Arjun doesn't take it)
Four years, Arjun. Inside the lattice. Do you know what's stored in there? Every carrier who died in the blast left a trace. Every trace has a last ninety seconds.
(beat, mild)
I experienced two hundred thousand deaths. Not once. On a loop. From the inside. For what I estimate was forty-one years of subjective time.
(beat)
So when I tell you I have studied human grief, I want you to understand I mean it *quantitatively.*

**ARJUN**
I pulled the ring.

**DEV**
Yes.

**ARJUN**
Say something.

**DEV**
(gently, and he means it entirely)
You were a boy who loved me and you couldn't stand still. I've had four decades to be angry with you and I used up about six weeks of it. The rest of the time I was working.
(he stands)
Which brings me to why you're here.

He gestures. The market square **holds**, and every person in it turns to face them at the same instant. Two hundred faces. Perfectly calm. Perfectly still.

**DEV (CONT'D)**
It's called the Concord. The Cradle can lace every nervous system on this coast into one standing field. Eight million, in the first pass. Everything else within a year.
(beat)
And in that field, I sever one function. Not memory — I'm not a vandal. Just the *grief* channel. The part that keeps the wound open after the wound is closed.
(beat)
No vendetta. No widow lying awake at three. No boy in a shaft doing something unforgivable because he can't bear to lose one man.
(gently)
Nobody would ever have to be you again, Arjun.

Silence. The gulls keep going.

**ARJUN**
And what's left?

**DEV**
Everything that isn't pain.

**ARJUN**
That's not an answer, that's a *brochure.*
(he steps in)
You want me to hand you the last kid on this coast so you can turn eight million people into the Anjari flats. Quiet. Clean. Nothing coming back.

Something moves across Dev's face. He genuinely hadn't heard that comparison before, and it genuinely bothers him — for about two seconds.

**DEV**
(quiet)
Her name is Kira. You could say her name.

**ARJUN**
You want your own daughter for a *component.*

**DEV**
I want my daughter to stop being a girl who watched her mother die. I can *do* that. I'm the only man in history who can do that.
(beat, and here the mask slips a millimetre)
And I don't remember Amara's face. I took mine out first — I wasn't going to sell a cure I hadn't taken. I know I loved her. I have the *fact*. The rest is a hole shaped like a woman.
(he looks at the orange in his hand)
It doesn't hurt at all. It's marvellous.

Arjun looks at him with total horror — because he is looking at the only man alive who understands exactly what Arjun's condition feels like, and who chose it.

**ARJUN**
No.

**DEV**
(nodding, unsurprised, almost warm)
Then I'll take her, and afterwards she won't mind, and neither will you, because you'll have forgotten this conversation by autumn.
(he sits back down on the wall)
Give it two minutes. The market gets busy. It's the best part.

**SMASH CUT — Arjun wakes in the boat, grey rain, and vomits over the side.**

### SCENE 18 — **PLOT POINT II**
**EXT. TOWER SIX ROOF — THE KITE-FIELD (LOC-10) — GOLDEN MONSOON BREAK**

> **OBJECTIVE:** Kira wants eleven minutes of being seventeen. Arjun wants to tell her the truth about the Silence. Sera wants the girl.
> **CONFLICT:** The film's only happy scene is the trap.
> **OUTCOME:** Sera takes Kira. Arjun's power fails him — again — against mirrors.
> **VALUE SHIFT:** Hope (+) → Loss (−−).

**The rain stops.** Eleven minutes of low golden light through the haze — the only warm exterior in the film so far. The Stiltway roofs come alive: forty kids, sixty kites, laundry snapping, pigeons up.

Kira, on the red water-tank, flies her saffron-and-black kite one-handed and pulls a tether with the other, weaving the two — the first time she uses her power for *fun.*

Arjun sits on the parapet with the notebook open, trying to write a sentence he can't finish. We read the attempts:

> *Kira — I need to tell you*
> ~~*Kira — the market at Anjari*~~
> ~~*Kira — your mother*~~

**KIRA**
(not looking, calling down)
You're doing the face.

**ARJUN**
I have one face.

**KIRA**
You have two. There's diving-face and there's the one where you're about to say something that ruins the afternoon.
(she reels the kite in a long arc)
Don't. Have the afternoon. It's eleven minutes.

He closes the notebook.

**ARJUN**
Okay.

**KIRA**
(after a beat, quieter, still flying)
Did you know her? My mother.

**ARJUN**
(a long pause)
...I knew her. She used to make the whole Corps drink water. Went round with a jug like we were children.
(beat)
She had a red thread on her wrist.

Kira's hands go still on the line.

**KIRA**
I remember the thread.

And **that** is when the golden light goes out.

Nine Hollow come up over the roof edge on all four sides. Walking. Always walking. And **SERA VANCE**, dry in the rain-haze, a spiderweb crack across her left gauntlet.

**SERA**
Kira Okonkwo. Your father sent a boat.
(warm)
*Sent a boat.*

Arjun is already moving. He anchors and throws a bastion wall between Sera and the girl —

— and Sera walks *through* the reflection of it, mirror-plates flaring, and the wall's own energy hammers Arjun across the roof into the pigeon coops.

Kira fires six tethers. Sera copy-steps: three afterimages, three of the tethers bind nothing at all, and the real Sera arrives inside Kira's guard and puts two fingers on the girl's throat.

**SERA (CONT'D)**
It doesn't hurt. *Doesn't hurt.*

Arjun claws upright, palms down, and **anchors** — a hardlight floor crystallising across the whole roof to trap her feet.

Sera simply steps up onto the parapet.

The Hollow have Kira on the harvest sled in nine seconds. She fights. She screams. She gets one tether onto the red water-tank and holds — for four seconds — the tank groaning, her palms burning, her eyes finding Arjun's across the roof.

**KIRA**
ARJUN—

The tether snaps. Her palms open up. They take her.

Arjun runs to the roof edge and throws a bastion after the sled — his one absolute limitation, in the worst place: **it cannot move.** A perfect, useless wall of cyan light, hanging over empty air, forty metres behind a departing sled.

He holds it anyway. And holds it. And holds it. Until his nose is running blood and the light goes amber-orange and dies.

The kites keep flying. The golden light comes back for its last four minutes. It is obscene.

### SCENE 19 — **ALL IS LOST**
**EXT. UNDER THE STILTWAY — BHASKAR'S BOAT — NIGHT — RAIN**

> **OBJECTIVE:** Arjun wants to remember what just happened long enough to act on it.
> **CONFLICT:** He overdrew. The bill is due.
> **OUTCOME:** He loses three weeks, Riya's name, the clinic, and Ila's face.
> **VALUE SHIFT:** Broken (−) → Erased (−−).

Rain drumming on tarpaulin. A single bulb. Arjun sits on the boat's floor with his back against the hull, notebook open in both hands, and he is reading his own handwriting like a foreign language.

**Direct sound drops out.** We hear his heartbeat and a high thin tone. Micro-stutters in the cut. A four-frame repeat.

Riya comes down the ladder, soaked, insignia still on, and sees his face.

**RIYA**
Arjun. Hey. Hey.

He looks up at her, polite and pleasant and absolutely blank.

**ARJUN**
I'm sorry — are you the doctor?

Riya stops breathing.

**RIYA**
(carefully)
It's Riya.

**ARJUN**
(nodding, agreeable, checking the notebook, finding the page)
"Riya Sen. You loved her. She left in the rain. It was fair."
(he looks up, entirely sincere)
That's very unfair to you. I'm sorry. I don't have the — I have the sentence, I don't have the *woman.*

**RIYA**
(her voice does one thing, once, and then she gets it back under control)
Okay.

**ARJUN**
(turning pages, faster now, and now there's fear in it)
There's a clinic. Ward 12. Meera. Did I go there? There's a — there was a girl, there's a girl with —
(he can't find it)
There's a name in here three times and I can't see her face. Riya. I can't see her *face*, and I wrote her down three times, which means she must have—

He stops. He puts the notebook down very carefully, like it's full of water.

**ARJUN (CONT'D)**
(quietly)
Who did I lose today?

**RIYA**
(sitting down beside him on the wet floor, not touching him)
Her name is Kira. She's seventeen. She flies kites and she robbed you and you've been teaching her the three laws.
(beat)
And she's been taken, and we're going to go and get her.

**ARJUN**
Okay.
(beat)
Say it again in a minute. I'll get it eventually. It sticks if you say it enough.

She says it again. And again. Sixteen times, through the night, in the bottom of a boat in the rain, while he writes it down.

### SCENE 20
**INT. WARD 12 — BURNT (LOC-04) — NIGHT — RAIN THROUGH THE ROOF**

> **OBJECTIVE:** Meera wants to give Arjun the only weapon that works, knowing what it costs.
> **CONFLICT:** She has to talk him into total self-erasure, and he says yes far too quickly.
> **OUTCOME:** The anchor procedure. The plan. Arjun's decision.
> **VALUE SHIFT:** Helpless (−) → A price he can pay (+/−).

The clinic is a black shell. Rain falls through the burnt roof into the dead aquarium. Ten thousand index cards are a grey smear on the floor. Meera stands in it, one-armed, bandaged, holding the warped class photograph.

**MEERA**
Eleven thousand four hundred people's memories. I kept them because their families couldn't.
(she toes the ash)
He'd say that's the disease. He'd say look how much of you is a filing cabinet for pain.
(beat)
The horrible thing, Arjun, is that most days I don't have an argument.

**ARJUN**
Give me the plan.

Meera puts a copper disc on the bench. His stabiliser.

**MEERA**
The Concord needs three things: the Cradle, the Loom, and a tether to weave the field. If we can't stop the first two, we take the load.
(she turns the disc over)
Your stabiliser slows the current going *into* your lattice. Reverse the polarity and it stops discriminating. You become a **ground.** Every joule the Loom pushes into eight million people, you take through yourself, and you put it in the mud.

**RIYA**
Will he survive it?

**MEERA**
(to Riya, not sparing her)
His heart, probably. His lattice, no. And every memory he's ever consolidated goes down the wire with it.
(to Arjun, finally looking at him)
Not the last month. Not since the accident. *All* of it. Ila. The Corps. Me. Her.
(beat)
You'd wake up as a man with a working body and no past whatsoever. You would not know your own name until someone told you, and then you'd know it the way you know a fact.

Silence. Rain in the ash.

**ARJUN**
Okay.

**MEERA**
(sharply)
No. Don't do that. Don't be *quick* about it—

**ARJUN**
Meera.
(he picks up the disc)
I've been paying this bill in instalments for twelve years for nothing at all. You're offering me a chance to spend the whole thing on something.
(beat)
That's not a sacrifice. That's a *bargain.*

**MEERA**
(and she breaks, finally, after 61 years of holding)
I built the Threadwork. I built you. I hid her. Every single thing that is about to happen came out of my hands.

**ARJUN**
(he takes her brass hand in both of his — the only real embrace in the film)
Then build me one more thing.

> **CONFLICT:** Riya has no legal case, only a correct one.

**RIYA**
Eight million people. Forty minutes from now.

**OSE**
On the word of a carrier with no memory, a dead scientist's theory, and a ghost.

**RIYA**
Yes.

**OSE**
(she almost smiles)
Do you know what happens if I send six skiffs to an oil rig and I'm wrong? The Authority loses the delta. Not the argument — the *delta*. There are four militias waiting for us to look stupid.

Then in an hour nobody will remember that I didn't.

### SCENE 22
**INT. THE ARK — ASSEMBLY HALL & MOON POOL (LOC-08) — NIGHT**

> **OBJECTIVE:** Dev wants his daughter to say yes.
> **CONFLICT:** He is offering her the one thing she wants, and he isn't lying about any of it.
> **OUTCOME:** She almost takes it.
> **VALUE SHIFT:** Defiance (+) → Temptation (−).

Bone-white. Silent. Windless. Shadowless. Thirty-metre ceilings and nothing on any wall. Two hundred Hollow stand in absolute stillness at the edges. In the centre, a 40 m circular Moon Pool opens onto black water.

Kira, barefoot in a white shift, six braids, jewellery removed to a small white dish. Her palms are bandaged. Arjun's brass regulator is still clipped at her waist — the one dirty object in the frame.

**DEV** walks to her. Barefoot. Crystallisation now takes the left jaw, neck, shoulder and chest **[STAGE 3]**. He stops at a respectful distance and does something devastating: he doesn't try to touch her.

**DEV**
You've got her hands. She had these terrible strong hands, and she'd—
(he stops, frustrated with himself)
No. I've got the sentence. I don't have the picture. I'm sorry, that's my own fault.

**KIRA**
(shaking, furious, seventeen)
You left me in the ash.

**DEV**
I was *in* the ash. For four years. I came out in sixty-three and Meera Sanyal had made you disappear, and she was right to.
(beat)
I have been building this for eight years so that the first thing I ever gave you would be worth having.

He raises a hand. The Moon Pool's black water goes still as glass, and **the Concord blooms on it** — a projection, but not a fake one; it's built from the Cradle's records.

**The Anjari market, 3 August 2059, 14:11.** And in it: **AMARA OKONKWO** — tall, close-cropped hair, medic's kit, red thread on her wrist — laughing at something off-frame, holding a five-year-old on her hip.

Kira makes a sound that is not a word.

**DEV (CONT'D)**
(quietly, beside her, both watching)
It's not a ghost and I won't insult you by calling it her. It's ninety seconds of a real afternoon.
(beat)
But when the Concord runs, every mind on this coast joins one field. And in a shared field, a recording isn't a recording. It's a *place.* You could go and stand in it. So could everyone who lost someone. Forever, and it would never once hurt.

**KIRA**
And the price.

**DEV**
You'd lose the ache. That's all. You keep the thread, the collar, the sound of her — you lose the part that folds you in half at three in the morning.
(beat)
I'm not asking you to forget your mother, Kira. I'm asking you to stop being *punished* for having had one.

Kira watches her mother laugh on the black water. Her bandaged hand comes up towards the image. Gold light stirs in her fingers, unbidden — she is *already weaving*, involuntarily.

**DEV (CONT'D)**
(the gentlest line in the film)
You're allowed to want it.

**KIRA**
(barely audible)
I want it.

**DEV**
I know.

Off her face — lit white, gold beginning at her fingertips — **CUT TO BLACK.**

### SCENE 23
**INT. THE DROWNED CATHEDRAL / EXT. BHASKAR'S YARD — PRE-DAWN**

> **OBJECTIVE:** Get three Corps mantles off a rusted rack eighteen metres underwater and make them fit.
> **CONFLICT:** Time, storm, and a man writing his own last page.
> **OUTCOME:** Suit-up. The notebook's final entry.
> **VALUE SHIFT:** Ordinary (−) → Guardians (+).

**UNDERWATER.** The green god-ray. The terrazzo hexagon. Arjun cuts the chain on the rack of Corps mantles. Three come free in a slow cloud of silt: a Bastion cuirass, a Tether harness, and a third, smaller one they take for Meera and never use.

**EXT. BHASKAR'S YARD — PRE-DAWN.** Sparks. Rivets. Bhaskar fits the salt-pitted cuirass to Arjun's chest and replaces three rivets with mismatched steel ones. Meera, one-handed, re-channels the forearm bracers so the conduits align with Arjun's actual Threadwork scars. Riya, insignia torn off, loads Nullpins she may not be able to use on anything.

Nobody speaks for ninety seconds of screen time. Just work, rain, and a storm coming in from the north.

Then Arjun steps away, sits on an upturned crate, and opens the notebook for the last time.

**INSERT — LOCKED-OFF MACRO, DIRECTLY OVERHEAD.** His hand. Wet paper. Pencil.

> *Read this first.*
>
> *You won't know me. That's all right — I didn't know most of me either.*
>
> *Your name is Arjun Vedh. You were a Guardian. You did a terrible thing when you were twenty because you loved someone, and today you are doing a good thing for the same reason, so it evens out. Don't check the arithmetic.*
>
> *The woman with the braid is Riya Sen. Believe her about everything.*
> *The woman with the brass arm is Meera. She is not to blame. Tell her that first, she'll need it early.*
> *The girl is Kira Okonkwo. She is seventeen. She flies kites. She is better at this than I was.*
>
> *You did a good thing. You won't remember it. That's allowed.*

He tears the page out — the way he always does — and then, for the first time in the film, **puts it back in.** Closes the book. Ties the red boot-lace. Hands it to Riya.

**ARJUN**
Don't give it to me until it's over.

**RIYA**
And if it isn't over?

**ARJUN**
(pulling the mantle on)
Then read it to yourself and skip the last line.

### SCENE 25
**EXT. THE ARK — LOWER DECK & LEG THREE — DAWN — STORM**

> **OBJECTIVE:** Get a boarding party onto a rig eighty metres above the sea.
> **CONFLICT:** Sixty Hollow, a storm, and Kira's freeze.
> **OUTCOME:** They board. Bhaskar's nephew dies. Riya holds the flank.
> **VALUE SHIFT:** Outside (−) → Inside, and paying (−).

Grapples, cargo nets, a dredger crane arm bent against Leg Three. Arjun anchors on a swinging net and builds **stairs** out of hardlight up the leg — hexagon by hexagon, each one costing him — while four hundred boat people climb them in the rain.

This is the film's biggest crowd image: ordinary people climbing a staircase of light in a category-five storm.

Hollow on the deck rail above, calm, dropping resonance charges. Riya, braid soaked, orange vest, coordinates the flank with a bullhorn and no rank.

**RIYA**
Nets to the left leg! Nobody climbs alone, everybody climbs roped!

A resonance charge takes out a section of stair. Arjun rebuilds it mid-fall, catches nine people, loses one — **BHASKAR'S NEPHEW, 19** — into the black water. Bhaskar sees it. Bhaskar keeps climbing. The film does not cut away from his face.

Above, on the upper deck: Kira, in the white shift, at the rail — free, unguarded, *choosing to be here* — sees the staircase of cyan light coming up the leg with four hundred people on it.

Her father, beside her, sees it too.

**DEV**
(genuinely moved)
That's the most Guardian thing I have ever seen.
(beat, to a Hollow)
Don't fire on the civilians. Not one.

### SCENE 26
**INT. THE ARK — ASSEMBLY HALL — DAWN**

> **OBJECTIVE:** Sera wants to hold the hall for six minutes. Arjun needs to be at the Moon Pool in four.
> **CONFLICT:** His power is structurally useless against her. Again.
> **OUTCOME:** He beats her by stopping trying to hit her.
> **VALUE SHIFT:** Blocked (−) → Through (+).

A white cathedral of nothing. Sera Vance walks out into the middle of it, mirror-plates, cracked left gauntlet, resonance blade drawn.

**SERA**
You've done this twice. It went badly twice.
(pleasantly)
*Badly twice.*

**ARJUN**
I know.

He throws a bastion anyway. She reflects it and it takes him off his feet and thirty metres down the hall. He gets up. Throws another. Reflected. Down again. Blood in his teeth.

Three copy-steps come at him from three angles and each lands a cut: forearm, thigh, and a resonance-blade slash across the cuirass that opens a conduit channel and makes his left arm go dead.

He crawls. She follows at a walk, in no hurry, and she is *narrating* to him kindly.

**SERA (CONT'D)**
You keep trying to put a wall between yourself and a mirror.
(beat)
*Between yourself and a mirror.*

And Arjun — face down on white lacquer, one arm dead — starts to laugh. Once. Painfully.

**ARJUN**
Third law. Never shield what you can move.

He puts his good palm flat, not in front of her —

**— on the floor.**

He anchors, and then **inverts** the bastion, and the ten square metres of decking Sera is standing on is not reinforced but *unsupported* — the hardlight plate he crystallises beneath it prises the deck plate up and flips it.

Sera drops through her own floor into the ballast void below — a fall she cannot reflect, because kinetic force was never on the menu.

Arjun crawls to the hole. Down in the dark, she's alive, wedged, mirror-plate cracked across the chest now, staring up at him. And in the cracked mirror of her own gauntlet, six inches from her face, is her own reflection, and she cannot look away from it.

For the first time, Sera Vance's calm is completely gone.

**SERA**
(and this is the only time she speaks with feeling)
Get me out.
(beat)
*Get me — I can see her. I can see* —
(she is looking at her own face)
— *who is that.*

Arjun looks down at a woman who has just met her own grief for the first time in three years.

**ARJUN**
(not cruelly)
That's you.

He leaves her the rope. He doesn't wait to see if she takes it.

---

### SCENE 27
**INT. THE ARK — MOON POOL — DAWN**

> **OBJECTIVE:** Dev needs Kira to weave. Kira needs to decide who she is.
> **CONFLICT:** Everything she wants is on one side and nothing but the truth is on the other.
> **OUTCOME:** She refuses. He fires the Concord anyway.
> **VALUE SHIFT:** Temptation (−) → Choice (+), then Catastrophe (−).

The black water. Amara laughing on it, on a loop, ninety seconds, forever.

Kira stands at the eight primary tether-lines, gold light already running up her forearms. Dev stands behind her, not touching, hands at his sides.

**DEV**
When you're ready. Not before.

Kira raises her hands. Six tethers. Then, for the first time — a seventh.

The room brightens.

And then: a sound that doesn't belong. Small. Tinny. Amber.

**THE ANCHOR-STONE**, skidding across white lacquer, thrown from the hall doorway by a bleeding man in a torn Bastion mantle.

It opens. And a shoulders-up volumetric of **Arjun** forms — recorded that morning at Bhaskar's yard, rain on his shoulders — reading aloud, awkwardly, from a notebook:

**ARJUN (RECORDING)**
Things I am afraid to forget. One: my sister Ila drew fish on my schoolbooks and the fish were bad. Two: Riya Sen puts her watch on backwards and I never asked why and now I never will.
(beat, and he looks straight into it)
Three: Kira Okonkwo is seventeen years old. She robbed me in the rain, and she was right about the rig, and she flies kites like it's an argument she's winning.
(beat)
That's it. That's the whole list. It's not a long list, and it *hurts*, and I would not trade one line of it.

The recording dissolves into grain.

Kira's seven tethers hang in the air, unwoven.

She reaches up, and — one at a time — takes the three copper clips out of the white dish and puts them back in her braids. Then the brass earring. Then she picks up Arjun's regulator from her waist and closes her fist around it.

**KIRA**
(to her father, and she is very calm)
I was five. I remember two things. Her collar, and no sound.
(beat)
That's not much. Everyone in that market got a whole life and I got a collar and a silence, and it is *mine*, and you don't get it.

**DEV**
Kira—

**KIRA**
You said you'd give me a place to stand in with her.
(beat)
You can't even remember her face.

Something in Dev Aranya goes very, very quiet.

**DEV**
(after a long moment, almost inaudible)
No. I can't.

He turns to the Concord spindle and puts his crystalline hand on it.

**DEV (CONT'D)**
I'm going to do it without you. It will be cruder. Some of them won't survive the pass.
(and here, finally, we hear grief in a man who deleted his own)
I've been building this for eight years. I don't know how to be a person who *stops.*

The Ark's 144 spines light. A tone climbs out of the sub-bass.

**FORTY KILOMETRES AWAY:** the Stiltway. A mother stops mid-sentence. A boy's hands go slack on a kite line. Four hundred faces on the Long Walk go smooth and calm at the same instant, and stop.

The Concord has started.

---

### SCENE 28 — **CLIMAX A**
**INT. THE CRADLE LOOM (LOC-09)**

> **OBJECTIVE:** Arjun must stop a man who is stronger, cannot be killed, and is not wrong.
> **CONFLICT:** Force has never worked and will not start now.
> **OUTCOME:** Arjun gives Dev back his grief.
> **VALUE SHIFT:** Losing (−) → Dev broken open (+/−).

A 200-metre geode. Standing crystal filament floor to ceiling like the strings of a stadium-sized harp. A brass ring platform at the centre. Everything is emissive white-blue, rising. 38 Hz in every frame.

Dev stands at the spindle, weaving eight million people by hand, crudely, and it is killing him — the crystallisation has taken his left hand entirely and is **cracking** **[STAGE 4]**.

Arjun walks the catwalk. Torn mantle, dead left arm, blood.

He builds a bastion. Dev makes a *room* out of light around it and the bastion shatters like a teacup.

**DEV**
(not unkindly, not stopping work)
I taught you that.

Arjun anchors and drives a bastion up through the platform. Dev unmakes it with a gesture.

**DEV (CONT'D)**
And that.

Arjun stops.

He walks the last twenty metres with his hands open, and Dev — because he cannot not — turns to look at him.

**ARJUN**
You're right.

**DEV**
(a flicker)
Don't.

**ARJUN**
You're right about all of it. I've had twelve years of your cure by accident. No anniversaries. No faces. I wake up light as a bird.
(beat)
It's the worst thing that has ever happened to anyone, Dev, and I'm the only man alive who can tell you that from the *inside*, and you never once asked me.

**DEV**
(hands still on the spindle)
Because the alternative is the shaft. The alternative is a boy who loved me pulling a ring.

**ARJUN**
Yes.
(beat)
I'm going to give you something. You can put it down after.

And he holds up **the anchor-stone.**

Not as a weapon. Open on his palm, the way you'd offer food.

**ARJUN (CONT'D)**
The Cradle recorded everyone who died in the blast radius. Every trace. Ninety seconds each.
(beat)
Amara was a carrier, Dev. She's in here. All of her. The whole afternoon.
(beat)
You took yours out first because you wouldn't sell a cure you hadn't taken. I'm giving it back so you know what you're selling.

**DEV**
(and now, at last, he is afraid)
Arjun. Don't.

Arjun presses the stone against the crystalline plate of Dev's chest.

**IT OPENS.**

**AND THE FILM GOES SILENT AND WARM:** the Anjari market, 14:11, real, unedited, from Amara's own recorded trace. Her hands. The red thread. A five-year-old on her hip complaining about mangoes. Her voice saying *"Dev, she's heavy, she's your daughter, come and take her—"* and turning, laughing, towards camera—

— and then, from inside her own last ninety seconds, the white light.

**BACK IN THE LOOM.**

Dev Aranya, 58, commander of the Guardian Corps, gets it back. All of it. At once. After eight years of a hole shaped like a woman.

He makes a sound no human being should make.

He falls to his knees on the brass ring, both hands over his face — one flesh, one crystal — while eight million minds hang half-woven above him.

**DEV**
(wrecked, to nobody)
Oh. Oh, *there* you are.

### SCENE 29 — **CLIMAX B / RESOLUTION**
**INT. THE CRADLE LOOM → EXT. THE ARK — SUNRISE**

> **OBJECTIVE:** Ground eight million people's worth of resonance.
> **CONFLICT:** The load has to go through a body.
> **OUTCOME:** Dev turns the polarity himself and dies. Arjun grounds the load and loses everything.
> **VALUE SHIFT:** Catastrophe (−) → Saved, at full price (+/−).

The Loom is climbing to white. The half-woven Concord field is unstable — it will either complete or collapse through every mind it's touching.

Meera, on the radio, distorted:

**MEERA (RADIO)**
Arjun, it has to go somewhere. If it collapses uncontrolled it takes them all with it. **Ground it.**

Arjun tears the mantle open and puts Meera's copper disc against his sternum. Reverses it.

Kira comes down the catwalk at a dead run.

**KIRA**
NO—

**ARJUN**
(turning, and he is completely calm, and he smiles at her, which is the shot that ends people)
Kira Okonkwo. Seventeen. Kites.
(beat)
See? I still had it.

Dev, on his knees, looks up — and does the last free thing he has.

**DEV**
Not him.

He puts both hands on the spindle and **reverses the Loom's polarity himself**, taking the first third of the load through his own crystal body so his daughter will never have to see the man who saved her die in front of her twice.

Dev Aranya burns from the inside out — white light through crystal, cracks racing across his chest, his face — and in the last two seconds it is clearly, unmistakably **his own face**, human, wrecked, awake.

He finds Kira in the light.

**DEV (CONT'D)**
Kira.
(and he gets it right)
Your mother's laugh. It came out of *you.*

He goes to ash and white light.

**AND THE REST OF THE LOAD COMES DOWN.**

Arjun anchors, both palms on bare crystal, and **grounds it.**

Every filament in his body lights at once. His conduit channels blow out. The cyan goes to white, then past white. The resonance pours down through him, through the crystal shelf, into the Cradle, into the mud, into the sea — a column of light forty kilometres wide that we see from the Stiltway, from the Tidewall, from space.

**IN THE STILTWAY:** four hundred faces on the Long Walk come back on, all at once, mid-sentence. A mother finishes her sentence. A boy's hands close on a kite line.

**INSIDE ARJUN:** we go inside for exactly six seconds, in white — the notebook pages, Ila's fish, Riya in the rain, the noodle stall, Meera's brass hand, Bhaskar's gold tooth, a saffron kite — each one arriving and then simply **going out**, like windows in a tower at the end of a night.

The last one to go is Kira on the salt flats, laughing, mid-swing on a gold line.

Then nothing.

**EXT. THE ARK — SUNRISE.** The 144 spines go dark. The Loom's hum stops — and its absence is the loudest thing in the film.

Kira drags Arjun out onto the upper deck. He is breathing. His Threadwork is dead pewter. His eyes are open and pleasant and empty.

And **the storm breaks.**

Not dramatically. It just — thins, and tears, and there is a seam of gold, and then, for the first time in two hours and five minutes of film:

**SKY.**

Four hundred boat people on the deck of a bone-white rig, soaked, look up at blue.

Riya reaches Arjun and takes his face in both hands.

**RIYA**
Hey. Hey. Do you know me?

Arjun looks at her with great courtesy.

**ARJUN**
No.
(beat)
Should I be worried? You look like it's bad news.

Riya laughs. It comes out as a sob. Both. She presses her forehead to his.

**RIYA**
No. It's the best news anyone's ever had.

Meera, one-armed, sits down heavily on the wet deck next to them and starts crying in Bengali. Bhaskar takes his cap off.

Kira stands apart at the rail, watching the sun come up over ninety fishing boats, with her palms open and gold light still running quietly in her hands.

### SCENE 30 — **DENOUEMENT**
**EXT. THE STILTWAY & TOWER SIX ROOF — MORNING — FOUR MONTHS LATER — CLEAR**

> **OBJECTIVE:** Hand the film to its new owner.
> **CONFLICT:** None. Earned peace.
> **OUTCOME:** Kira is the Guardian. Arjun is free. The kites are in colour.
> **VALUE SHIFT:** Ending (+).

**Clear morning light.** The first fully clean sky. The Stiltway is loud, wet, alive, and **rebuilding**: the Long Walk's mid-span is new untreated bamboo, pale and bright against everything rusted around it.

Along the ropes, hundreds of memorial kites — **and this time they're coloured.** Saffron, teal, oxblood, magenta.

**KIRA OKONKWO (17)**, in a fitted Corps Tether mantle, full saffron cloak, six braids, three copper clips, brass regulator on her belt, stands on the new span with six gold tethers out — hauling a four-tonne girder into place while twenty workers guide it and a dozen kids run underneath screaming.

**BHASKAR**
(bellowing from below)
LEFT! LEFT, GIRL—

**KIRA**
(bellowing back, delighted)
IT *IS* LEFT!

On a jetty at water level: **ARJUN VEDH**, plain cotton shirt, sleeves rolled, barefoot, sitting with his hands on his knees, watching the water with the perfect calm of a man with nothing behind him. His Threadwork is dull pewter. It will never light again.

**RIYA SEN** sits down beside him with two glasses of chai. Green raincoat, no insignia, braid.

**RIYA**
Chai.

**ARJUN**
(sipping; makes a face)
It's very sweet.

**RIYA**
(deadpan, eyes front)
Yes. It is.

He accepts this. They sit.

Then she takes out **the notebook** — waterlogged, swollen, red boot-lace — and puts it in his hands.

**RIYA (CONT'D)**
You asked me to hold onto this until it was over.
(beat)
It's over.

Arjun turns it over. Opens it. Reads the last page — and we hear it in his own voice, from four months ago:

**ARJUN (V.O.)**
*Your name is Arjun Vedh. You were a Guardian... The girl is Kira Okonkwo. She is seventeen. She flies kites. She is better at this than I was.*
*You did a good thing. You won't remember it. That's allowed.*

He reads it twice. His face doesn't change much — he has no memory to attach it to. But his thumb goes over the last line, once.

**ARJUN**
(to Riya, genuinely curious, not sad)
Was I? Better or worse than this?

**RIYA**
(watching Kira haul four tonnes of steel through the air on threads of gold light)
Oh, you were *nowhere* near.

A shadow crosses them. Kira drops down onto the jetty, lands light, out of breath, grinning, and stands in front of him — the way you'd stand in front of someone you visit every day and who never remembers you came.

**KIRA**
Morning. I'm Kira.

**ARJUN**
(shaking her hand, polite, warm)
Arjun. Apparently.

**KIRA**
Yeah. Apparently.
(beat)
Come up on the roof. There's wind.

**EXT. TOWER SIX ROOF — CONTINUOUS.** The red water-tank. Kira's name scratched into it. The kite kids. The clean blue sky.

Kira puts a **kite spool** in Arjun's hands and steps back.

**KIRA (CONT'D)**
Hold the anchor. Let the corner argue.

Arjun looks down at the spool. Something in his face moves — not memory. The *shape* of memory. A man recognising the fit of a thing his hands know.

**ARJUN**
(quietly, to himself, surprised)
...Huh.

He lets the line out. The saffron kite goes up over the Stiltway, over ninety boats, over a rebuilt walkway, into a sky with nothing in it at all.

Kira, beside him, watching him and not the kite.

Hold. Hold.

**CUT TO BLACK.**

**TITLE CARD:**
> # THE LAST GUARDIAN

Under it, smaller, fading in three seconds later:
> *the first of the next.*

**END.**
