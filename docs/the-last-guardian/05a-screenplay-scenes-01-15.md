### SCENE 1
**INT. CRADLE CORE — CONTAINMENT SHAFT (LOC-01) — 3 AUGUST 2059 — 14:12**

> **SCENE OBJECTIVE:** Arjun (20) wants to reach Dev before the containment cascade completes.
> **CONFLICT:** Lockdown protocol, a 200 m drop, and Dev ordering him to leave.
> **OUTCOME:** Withheld — we cut 4 seconds before his hand reaches the lever.
> **VALUE SHIFT:** Safety (+) → Catastrophe (−). Audience question planted: *what did he do?*

Black. A low 38 Hz hum.

A woman's voice, warm, over black:

**AMARA (V.O.)**
Anchor before you build.

Red alarm gyros bloom out of the dark. We are 200 metres down a brass-ringed shaft, crystal filament growing through cracked concrete like frost. Klaxons. Steam. Sixty CORPS PERSONNEL running the catwalks.

CADET ARJUN VEDH (20 — clean-shaven, no grey, cadet half-plate) hauls himself onto Level 3 against the evacuation flow.

Below, pinned under a collapsed containment arm on the level-3 catwalk: **DEV ARANYA (46)**, full commander's regalia, oxblood cloak, wedding band on his hand. Calm as a Sunday.

**DEV**
Cadet. Go up.

**ARJUN**
Sir, I can lift the arm—

**DEV**
You can. And then the ring holds nine seconds instead of forty. Go *up*, Arjun.

A shockwave. The shaft lurches. Down at the base, a white light is being born — not a fireball. A *bloom*, soundless, patient, rising.

Arjun looks at the white light. Looks at Dev. Looks at the containment ring lever — brass, chest height, red grip, forty metres of catwalk away.

**DEV (CONT'D)**
Don't.

**ARJUN**
I'm sorry.

He runs. The lever fills frame. His hand reaches—

**HARD CUT TO BLACK.** Sound drops to absolute zero for three full seconds.

**TITLE CARD (white on black, silent):**
> *Two hundred thousand people died in four seconds.*
> *One hundred and thirty-eight Guardians died with them.*
> *Two walked out.*

### SCENE 2
**INT./UNDERWATER. THE DROWNED CATHEDRAL (LOC-02) — 2071 — DAY**

> **OBJECTIVE:** Arjun wants to strip 30 kg of copper bus-bar before his air runs out.
> **CONFLICT:** Low air, silt, and his own failing memory of the route out.
> **OUTCOME:** He gets the copper; the anchor-stone wakes.
> **VALUE SHIFT:** Routine (+) → Summoned (−).

Green-blue dusk, eighteen metres down. A single god-ray through a broken skylight. Cast-iron columns. A departure board frozen at 14:12, 3 AUG 2059.

ARJUN VEDH (32) works a bus-bar loose with a pry bar. Amber lamp on his mask. Threadwork lines silver-dormant on his forearms. He is completely at home here.

He checks a slate. Writes with a grease pencil: *EXIT — NORTH ARCH — THIRD COLUMN.*

He has written the same thing four times.

He pushes off — and stops. A slow amber pulse at his hip. The anchor-stone. Once. Twice. A heartbeat rhythm.

Arjun stares at it the way you'd stare at a phone ringing in an empty house.

**ARJUN (O.S., breath through the regulator)**
No.

He wraps his fist around it. The pulse continues through his fingers.

Above him, through the skylight shaft, the surface is a distant grey coin.

### SCENE 3
**EXT. THE STILTWAY — MARKET LEVEL & TOWER SIX ROOF (LOC-03/LOC-10) — LATE AFTERNOON**

> **OBJECTIVE:** Arjun wants to sell copper and buy a week of quiet. Kira wants a regulator she can sell for four hundred.
> **CONFLICT:** Each other.
> **OUTCOME:** She takes it. He lets her.
> **VALUE SHIFT:** Isolation (+) → Contact (−/+).

Rain. Nine stilt-towers, LED string lights, tarpaulin in saffron and teal, forty rope walkways stacked into the mist. Along the Long Walk, hundreds of **white paper kites** hang from the ropes — memorial kites. It is the twelfth anniversary week.

Arjun crosses in a storm-grey coat, copper on his shoulder, invisible by practice.

Above: a whip-crack of laughter. KIRA OKONKWO (17) runs the roof-edge of Tower Six barefoot, kite spool in hand, saffron-and-black kite cutting an arc through the rain. Six braids, three copper clips, forearms wrapped. Two smaller kids scream with delight behind her.

**KIRA**
(over her shoulder, not looking)
Line! LINE—

She cuts a rival kite. The Stiltway roars.

Down at BHASKAR'S YARD, Arjun trades copper with **BHASKAR "BASH" RELE (44)**, bald, grey moustache, gold tooth.

**BHASKAR**
Two-sixty.

**ARJUN**
It's four hundred of copper.

**BHASKAR**
It's four hundred of *stolen* copper. Two-sixty.
(beat, softer)
You eat today?

**ARJUN**
I'll eat.

Bhaskar puts a foil packet on the copper anyway.

Arjun turns — and a shoulder hits him, light as a bird. Kira, moving past, already six metres gone, already down a ladder.

His hand goes to his harness. The **regulator socket is empty**.

Arjun looks up. Kira is on the walkway above, holding his brass regulator, weighing it, entirely unashamed.

**KIRA**
You dive with a rig this old, you're going to die anyway. I'm helping.

**ARJUN**
That's four hundred rupees of my lungs.

**KIRA**
Then it's a *good* four hundred!

She's gone into the crowd. Arjun stands in the rain, and — this is important — a corner of his mouth moves. The first almost-smile in twelve years.

Then, on every screen strung above the market, an AUTHORITY BULLETIN: **CARRIER SWEEP — SECTOR NINE — COMPLIANCE IS PROTECTION.**

The market's noise drops by half.

### SCENE 4
**EXT. THE STILTWAY — MARKET LEVEL — DUSK**

> **OBJECTIVE:** Riya wants to run a clean sweep with zero discharges. Arjun wants to not exist.
> **CONFLICT:** A frightened crowd; a rookie with a Nullpin launcher.
> **OUTCOME:** One civilian pinned by mistake; Arjun keeps his hands in his pockets and hates himself.
> **VALUE SHIFT:** Order (+) → Complicity (−).

Six Kestrel skiffs drop onto the market deck. Authority riot units fan out — grey weave, oxblood chevrons, full visors, clumsy in the wet.

**COMMANDER RIYA SEN (34)** steps off skiff **#6**. Command coat, bun, gloves, watch face-inward. She does not raise her voice once in this scene.

**RIYA**
Wrists out, palms up, we're gone in ten minutes. Nobody's being arrested. Anyone who runs makes this longer for their neighbours.

A rookie, **OFFICER PANDE (22)**, jumpy, tracks a fruit-seller's sudden movement and fires. A descending metallic *swallow.* The Nullpin buries itself in the man's thigh. He screams — but no light comes out of him. He isn't a carrier at all.

Riya crosses, kneels, cuts the pin out herself, presses the wound.

**RIYA (CONT'D)**
(to Pande, flat)
Give me your launcher.
(he does)
Now hold his leg. You're going to sit with him until the medic comes, and you're going to learn his name.

She stands, scanning the crowd — and passes directly over a man in a grey coat, face wrapped, hands in his pockets.

Arjun. Two metres away. Not breathing.

Riya's eyes catch. Something almost registers.

**AUTHORITY RADIO**
Commander, Spire's asking for your position.

She turns away. Arjun walks. Behind him, his fists unclench and both hands are shaking.

### SCENE 5
**INT. WARD 12 — MEERA'S CLINIC (LOC-04) — NIGHT**

> **OBJECTIVE:** Arjun wants the stabiliser recalibrated and no conversation.
> **CONFLICT:** Meera insists on the conversation.
> **OUTCOME:** We learn the rules of Bleaching, the notebook, and that the anchor-stone is being pinged.
> **VALUE SHIFT:** Denial (+) → Dread (−).

Rain on a steel roof. Three welded containers. A wall of ten thousand index cards. An aquarium with no fish. **DR. MEERA SANYAL (61)**, brass prosthetic left arm, glasses on a chain, works at the back of Arjun's neck with a soldering iron.

**MEERA**
Hold still. [BENGALI] *Don't move, you great ox.*

**ARJUN**
How bad.

**MEERA**
Stabiliser's fine. You're not. When did you last write in the book?

He hands her the notebook. Waterlogged, swollen, elastic band. She opens it — we see the handwriting, all in the same careful hand:

> *Your sister was called Ila. She drew fish.*
> *You take chai with too much sugar. You will forget this and order it plain. Don't.*
> *Meera is your friend. She lost her arm because of the Corps.*
> *You are NOT ALLOWED to use the current.*
> *Riya Sen. You loved her. She left in the rain. It was fair.*

Meera turns a page. Then another. Then looks up.

**MEERA (CONT'D)**
You've torn out nine pages.

**ARJUN**
They stopped being true.

**MEERA**
That's not how—
(she stops, changes tack)
Arjun. Sit down. Properly.
(he does)
How much of last month.

**ARJUN**
(a long pause; entirely calm)
I know I dived. I know I ate. There's a woman at the noodle stall who calls me by a name I don't correct because I think it might be mine.

Meera puts down the iron. Her prosthetic hums amber with fatigue.

**MEERA**
It's accelerating.

He takes out the anchor-stone and puts it on the bench. It pulses. Amber. Once. Twice.

Meera goes absolutely still. Her prosthetic hand closes.

**MEERA (CONT'D)**
(very quietly)
Where did it do that.

**ARJUN**
Cathedral. Eighteen metres down. Meera — nothing can ping that. The Corps is *ash.*

**MEERA**
(not looking at him)
Then something in the Cradle is calling its hardware home.

### SCENE 6 — **INCITING INCIDENT**
**EXT. THE STILTWAY — THE LONG WALK (LOC-03) — DUSK — HEAVY RAIN**

> **OBJECTIVE:** The Hollow want to harvest three registered carriers quietly. Kira wants to get her kids off the walkway. Arjun wants to walk past.
> **CONFLICT:** Kira's power ignites in public; the walkway's mid-span fails with sixty people on it.
> **OUTCOME:** Arjun raises a bastion, saves four hundred lives, and is filmed.
> **VALUE SHIFT:** Hiding (+) → Exposed (−). **This is the point of no return.**

Rain hammering. A **HOLLOW HARVEST SLED** glides onto the market deck — bone-white, silent, twelve empty restraint frames. Nine HOLLOW step off. They do not shout. They walk.

**HOLLOW LEAD**
(pleasantly, to the whole market)
Three names. Everyone else may continue.

He reads three names off a white card. The third is a boy of twelve.

The Stiltway erupts. People run for the Long Walk — 120 metres of rope-and-plank between Towers Three and Six.

Kira is halfway across with the two kite kids. She turns back for the twelve-year-old.

A Hollow's hand closes on her wrist.

And Kira's hands **ignite gold-white** — brilliant, uncontrolled, six tethers firing at once from her palms. Five hundred people see it.

The tethers rip through the walkway's suspension ropes. The Long Walk's mid-span **fails** — forty metres of decking, sixty people, dropping eleven metres into black water and steel.

At the deck's edge, a man in a grey coat stops walking.

Close on Arjun's face. Rain. The screaming. His notebook line — *YOU ARE NOT ALLOWED* — flashes as an insert, one frame, subliminal.

**ARJUN**
(to himself)
Ah, hell.

He drops to one knee and slams both palms onto the deck.

**ANCHOR.** His Threadwork ignites cyan-white from C7 down both arms.

A hexagonal plane of hardlight **crystallises out of nothing** beneath the falling span, forty metres wide, catching sixty bodies, then locking upward into a full bastion bridge. The chime of it is enormous and beautiful.

Sixty people land on light. Four hundred more stop running.

Silence, except the rain on the shield.

Arjun kneels at the centre of it, both palms down, blood coming out of his left nostril, brow split by a flying rivet. He is holding up the entire mid-span of the Stiltway.

**ARJUN (CONT'D)**
(gritted, to the crowd)
GO. It's not going to be here long.

They go. They also **film it** — twenty phones, every angle.

Kira, on the far side, staring at him, tethers still burning in her hands.

Across her face: *there is another one of me.*

Arjun rises. The bastion holds for two seconds after he lifts his hands — then dissolves into light-dust, and the gap in the Long Walk is a gap forever.

He looks at Kira. Points at her, then at the water below.

**ARJUN (CONT'D)**
Run. Now. They've already got your face.

### SCENE 7
**INT./EXT. BASTION SPIRE OPS DECK 18 (LOC-06) / THE STILTWAY — NIGHT**

> **OBJECTIVE:** Riya wants to identify the bastion-class signature before Ose does. Ose wants a public arrest.
> **CONFLICT:** Riya recognises the shield and says nothing.
> **OUTCOME:** A citywide manhunt is authorised. Riya buries a file for the seventh time — and this time it doesn't work.
> **VALUE SHIFT:** Protection (+) → Exposure (−).

Forty monitors, rain-streaked glass, cyan fluorescents. The Stiltway footage plays on all of them: the hexagonal bridge blooming out of nothing.

Riya watches it eleven times. Her hand goes flat on the desk.

**MINISTER TALIA OSE (55)** enters — ivory high-collar coat, jade signet, silver-black chignon. She never raises her voice either. Nobody in this film raises their voice except civilians.

**OSE**
Class.

**RIYA**
Bastion. Structural. Static geometry, hexagonal tessellation, anchored discharge.

**OSE**
How many bastion-class survived the Silence?

**RIYA**
(a beat too long)
The register says none.

**OSE**
The register is a document, Commander. I asked how many *survived.*

Riya says nothing.

**OSE (CONT'D)**
(gently, worse than shouting)
Every year for six years, one carrier file fails to clear the archive. Every year I let it. I let it because you are the best officer I have and because grief makes people sentimental and sentiment is survivable.
(she turns to the window)
Four hundred people saw a Guardian this evening. By morning it will be four hundred million. Sentiment is no longer survivable.

**RIYA**
He saved sixty people.

**OSE**
The last time one of them saved people, Commander, we buried two hundred thousand.
(beat)
Bring him in. Alive, if you're quick.

### SCENE 8
**INT. WARD 12 — NIGHT**

> **OBJECTIVE:** Arjun wants Meera to shut the anchor-stone off.
> **CONFLICT:** It opens instead.
> **OUTCOME:** Dev's voice. Arjun refuses the call.
> **VALUE SHIFT:** Grief (+) → Terror (−).

Arjun, brow split and bleeding, sits in the dental chair. Kira, dragged along and furious, stands by the door with her arms crossed and a knife she thinks nobody has noticed.

**KIRA**
I don't need a doctor, I need a boat.

**MEERA**
(not looking up)
Sit down before I sedate you, child.

The anchor-stone on the bench pulses faster. Amber. Amber. Amber.

Then it **opens** — the brass cage unfolding like a seed head — and a shoulders-up volumetric of a man forms above it, edges dissolving into grain. Grey beard, broad shoulders, one crystalline eye.

Meera's tea tumbler hits the floor.

**DEV (RECORDING)**
Arjun.

Arjun stands so fast the chair goes over.

**DEV (RECORDING) (CONT'D)**
If you can hear this, the lattice has found you, which means you're alive, which means you got out. Good. That's good.
(a warm, exhausted smile)
I'm not angry. I have never been angry with you. I want you to write that down, because I know you won't keep it.
(beat)
I've had a long time to think, and I've finished the thinking. I've found the way to make it stop hurting. All of it. For everyone. It isn't a metaphor, Arjun, it's *engineering.*
(beat)
Come home. Bring nothing.

The volumetric collapses. The stone goes dull.

Silence. Rain on the roof.

**KIRA**
(small, for once)
Who was that?

**MEERA**
(hollowed out)
That was the man who built the Guardian Corps.

**ARJUN**
(already at the door)
No.

**MEERA**
Arjun—

**ARJUN**
No. I'm a salvage diver. I pull copper. I have four hundred rupees and a notebook and I am *not* the person that man is talking to, because that person doesn't exist anymore, Meera, you *saw* to that—

He stops. Because he's said something cruel, and because he can see it land in her prosthetic hand.

**ARJUN (CONT'D)**
(quiet)
I'm sorry.

Outside, in the rain, something white glides past the window. Silent.

### SCENE 9 — **PLOT POINT I**
**INT./EXT. WARD 12 — CONTINUOUS — NIGHT**

> **OBJECTIVE:** Sera wants Kira, intact and calm. Arjun wants everyone out.
> **CONFLICT:** Arjun's power is useless against Sera frontally; Meera won't leave her records.
> **OUTCOME:** The clinic burns, Meera is stabbed, Arjun takes Kira and runs.
> **VALUE SHIFT:** Sanctuary (+) → Nothing left (−).

The clinic door doesn't break — it is **unbolted**, politely, from outside.

**SERA VANCE (29)** steps in out of the rain. Bone-white lacquer, mirrored eyes, platinum crown braid, absolutely dry.

**SERA**
Kira Okonkwo. You're expected. It doesn't hurt, and afterwards you won't mind that it happened.
(a genuine, warm smile)
*Won't mind that it happened.*

**ARJUN**
Walk out.

He throws a bastion — a wall, slamming across the room.

Sera raises a mirror gauntlet. The bastion's own force returns along its vector and puts **Arjun** through the pegboard wall. Brass instruments everywhere.

**SERA**
Bastion class. That's very rare now.
(stepping over him)
*Very rare now.*

Meera swings the surgical lamp into Sera's head. Sera takes it, turns, and puts the resonance blade through Meera's right shoulder — clean, unhurried, no anger in it at all.

Kira **screams** and six tethers fire wild, wrapping the aquarium, the lamp rig and the ceiling gantry. She yanks. The whole gantry comes down on Sera, sparks into the coolant tank, and the clinic catches.

Arjun, on his knees in burning index cards — ten thousand people's memories curling into ash around him — grabs Meera under her prosthetic arm.

**MEERA**
The cards—

**ARJUN**
They're gone.

**MEERA**
(shouting, terrible)
THEY'RE NOT *CARDS*—

Kira drags her other side. They go out through the roof hatch into the rain.

Behind them, under a burning gantry, Sera Vance sits up. There is a hairline crack across her left gauntlet. She looks at her own reflection in it and, for the first time, her calm slips — she turns the gauntlet away from her face.

### SCENE 10
**EXT. THE TIDEWALL — SLUICE GATE 31 (LOC-07) — DAWN — DRIVING RAIN**

> **OBJECTIVE:** Get Meera to the north-side field hospital and get across the wall.
> **CONFLICT:** Two Kestrel skiffs; a jammed sluice; Arjun cannot make anything that moves.
> **OUTCOME:** They cross. Riya sees Arjun's face on thermal and does not fire.
> **VALUE SHIFT:** Pursued (−) → Escaped, exiled (−/+).

Forty metres of concrete and rusted steel teeth. Searchlights raking the swell. A sign: **NORTH OF THIS LINE — NO RECOVERY.**

Bhaskar's boat, overloaded, runs the sluice channel. Meera unconscious across the thwarts. Kira on the tiller because she's the only one who can see in this.

Two Kestrels come down the wall line. **Skiff #6** leads — Riya, canopy-less, soaked, one hand on the launcher rail.

**RIYA (RADIO)**
Target's in the channel. Hold fire, we're over civilians.

**PANDE (RADIO)**
Ma'am, Spire says—

**RIYA (RADIO)**
*Hold fire.*

The second skiff fires anyway. Nullpins skate off the water. Arjun anchors on the bow and throws up a bastion plate — the pins go into it and *stick*, quivering, like darts in a board.

The cost hits instantly: his nose opens, and he says, to nobody—

**ARJUN**
Where's — the girl's name. What's the girl's name.

**KIRA**
(screaming over the engine)
KIRA! IT'S KIRA, YOU ABSOLUTE—

They hit the half-open Sluice Gate 31 at speed. Steel screams. The boat scrapes through with fifteen centimetres to spare, taking the port gunwale off.

Riya's skiff pulls up hard at the gate. Her left fan cowling clips the concrete and **cracks** — a mark it will carry for the rest of the film.

Thermal scope. Riya sees, in green-white, a man kneeling on a bow with light coming out of his forearms.

She sees his face.

Her thumb comes off the trigger.

**RIYA**
(to herself, very quietly)
...you idiot.

**PANDE (RADIO)**
Commander? Do we pursue north?

**RIYA (RADIO)**
(beat)
Negative. North of the wall is no recovery. Log it.

She turns the skiff. On the far side of the gate, Arjun's grey coat, snagged on a steel tooth, flaps in the rain and stays behind — he never wears it again.

### SCENE 11
**EXT. ANJARI SALT FLATS (LOC-05) — "DAY" — NO WIND**

> **OBJECTIVE:** Arjun wants to teach Kira enough control to survive being hunted. Kira wants to know what he is.
> **CONFLICT:** He teaches like a man handing over a loaded weapon; she learns like a thief.
> **OUTCOME:** The three laws. Her first controlled weave. His first honest sentence in twelve years.
> **VALUE SHIFT:** Alone (−) → Two of them (+).

**The sound floor drops to nothing.** Ninety kilometres of bleached, cracked white salt. Ghost-foundations. A half-melted transmission pylon leaning on the horizon. Flat white sky, three stops brighter than the whole film so far.

No rain. No wind. No birds. **No sound at all** except their footsteps, which are too loud.

Kira turns a full circle, appalled.

**KIRA**
Why is it so *quiet*—

**ARJUN**
It's always like this. Nothing's come back.

They set Meera down in the shade of a bleached bus shell. Kira paces the outline of a vanished building — a low rectangle in the salt.

She is standing, though neither of them knows it, in the exact footprint of the Anjari market square where her mother died.

**ARJUN (CONT'D)**
Give me your hands.
(she doesn't)
Kira. You put six lines out in a crowd and dropped a walkway. If you don't learn to close your hands, you'll kill someone you like.

She gives him her hands. He turns them over — the gold hairlines run from her knuckles to her elbows now, permanent.

**ARJUN (CONT'D)**
Three laws. They're the only thing I kept.
(beat)
**Anchor before you build.** Whatever you make, it pulls on you first. If you're not fixed to something, the current takes you instead.
(he sets her feet with his boot, adjusts her shoulders)
**Never shield what you can move.** Don't spend yourself on a thing you could just carry out of the way. Most heroism is furniture-moving done badly.

**KIRA**
And the third?

**ARJUN**
(a long pause)
**The current takes payment in you.** Always. Every time. There's no version where it doesn't.

**KIRA**
What does it take from you?

He doesn't answer. He unclips the notebook, ties it shut with a **red boot-lace**, and puts it back in his chest pouch.

**KIRA (CONT'D)**
(getting it)
...Oh.

She holds out the stolen brass regulator. He looks at it.

**ARJUN**
Keep it. I'm not diving this week.

She clips it to her belt. It stays there for the rest of the film.

**MONTAGE — TRAINING.** Kira anchoring to the pylon. Kira snapping tethers and burning her palms. Kira braiding two lines into one and lifting the bus shell fifteen centimetres. Kira, at the end, running fifty metres and swinging out over the salt on a single gold line, laughing — the first laugh in the film — while Arjun watches, and the watching costs him something.

That night, by a driftwood fire, Meera awake and grey-faced:

**MEERA**
Teach her the fourth law.

**ARJUN**
There is no fourth law.

**MEERA**
There is. Dev taught it to you and you crossed it out of the book because you didn't like it.
(to Kira)
*Never guard something you can't afford to lose.*

Arjun looks into the fire and doesn't argue.

### SCENE 12
**EXT. ANJARI SALT FLATS — LATER — "DAY"**

> **OBJECTIVE:** Riya wants to bring Arjun in. Arjun wants twelve hours. Both are lying about what they actually want.
> **CONFLICT:** Eight years of unfinished argument, conducted in logistics.
> **OUTCOME:** She trades her silence for the truth about the Cradle ping.
> **VALUE SHIFT:** Enemy (−) → Uneasy ally (+).

Skiff #6 sits on the salt, cracked fan cowling, engine ticking. Riya stands twenty metres out with her hands visible and empty. Kira watches from the bus shell with six lines half-lit.

**RIYA**
I've got a warrant, four hours of fuel, and no partner, because I told Pande the scope was broken.

**ARJUN**
Riya.

**RIYA**
(fast, brittle)
Don't do the thing where you say my name like you're checking it against a list.

Silence. The dead air of the flats swallows it.

**RIYA (CONT'D)**
Sixty people. On camera. After twelve years of being *very* good at not existing.

**ARJUN**
The walkway went. There was nobody else.

**RIYA**
There's never anybody else, that's the whole—
(stops, resets, goes procedural)
The Hollow hit a registered clinic in Ward 12 last night. Authority didn't sanction it. That's the fourth non-sanctioned harvest this month and Ose has stopped asking who's driving the sleds.
(beat)
What's on the north coast, Arjun?

He takes out the anchor-stone and tosses it. She catches it. It pulses amber in her palm and she flinches — she's seen these before, on the bodies at Anjari, when she was nineteen and pulling them out of the salt for six weeks.

**ARJUN**
Something in the Cradle is calling Corps hardware home. And it's using Dev Aranya's voice.

**RIYA**
Dev Aranya is a *ghost story*. Boat people tell it to—
(she looks at the stone. Looks at his face.)
God. You believe it.

**ARJUN**
I need to see the inquiry. The real one. INQ-0803-59.

**RIYA**
That's in Archive B7 behind a ministerial seal.

**ARJUN**
Yes.

**RIYA**
You're asking me to burn my career to show you a *file*.

**ARJUN**
I'm asking you to show me ninety seconds I don't have.
(beat, and this is the most naked he gets)
Riya. I don't know what I did.

A long look. The flats are silent enough that we hear her glove tighten on the stone.

**RIYA**
(finally, businesslike, because that's how she survives)
Oh-three-forty tomorrow. Archive shift change is eleven minutes. You get the girl a coat, because you look like kidnappers.

She walks back to the skiff, then stops without turning.

**RIYA (CONT'D)**
The noodle stall woman calls you Vikram. I know because I paid her to keep the tab open.
(beat)
Eight years, Arjun.

She flies. Kira, from the bus shell:

**KIRA**
Who's Vikram?

**ARJUN**
(watching the skiff go)
Apparently me.

### SCENE 13
**EXT. ANJARI SALT FLATS — NIGHT / INT. CRADLE CORE (FLASH) — 2059**

> **OBJECTIVE:** Arjun wants to sleep. The stone wants to show him something.
> **CONFLICT:** His memory is a locked room and the stone has a key that only half fits.
> **OUTCOME:** A fragment: Dev's mentorship, and four frames of the lever.
> **VALUE SHIFT:** Numbness (+) → The wound reopening (−).

Firelight on white salt. Kira asleep against Meera. Arjun holds the anchor-stone in both hands.

### SCENE 15 — **MIDPOINT: THE TRUTH**
**INT. ARCHIVE B7 — PLAYBACK BOOTH / INT. CRADLE CORE — 2059**

> **OBJECTIVE:** Arjun wants to see the ninety seconds. Every part of him also wants not to.
> **CONFLICT:** None. This is the scene where the film stops fighting and just tells the truth.
> **OUTCOME:** He caused the Silence. He killed 200,000 people, 138 Guardians, Ila, and Kira's mother.
> **VALUE SHIFT:** Survivor (+) → Cause (−−−). **The floor of the film drops out.**

A brass helmet-footage reader. The cartridge clicks in. A small square screen, 4:3, inside our 2.39 frame — the aspect ratio itself is a jail.

**SCENE 1'S EXACT SHOTS REPLAY** — identical blocking, identical lighting, identical lens. Dev pinned. *"Cadet. Go up."* The white bloom rising. The lever.

And this time we do not cut.

Arjun's hand closes on the brass lever with the red grip. He hauls it. The containment ring **retracts** — freeing Dev, and unsealing the Cradle.

For one second, it works. Dev pulls himself free and looks up at the boy with something like pride and something like horror.

**DEV (2059)**
(on the footage, tiny, tinny)
Oh, Arjun. What have you done.

Then the bloom comes up the shaft.

It is not fire. It is silent, white, and *gentle*. It takes the catwalks. It takes Dev. It takes the helmet cam's frame, and the last thing recorded is the boy's own reflection in his shattered visor, mouth open, lit white.

The footage ends. The little screen holds a white frame. Then black.

**CUT TO: THE PLAYBACK BOOTH.** Four faces in the dark.

Arjun does not move for eleven seconds. Then he reaches, very calmly, for his notebook, opens it to the boxed sentence — *ASK: WHO PULLED THE RING?* — and answers it in pencil:

> *You did.*

**RIYA**
Arjun—

**ARJUN**
(perfectly level, which is worse)
Two hundred thousand.

**RIYA**
You were twenty years old and you were trying to save your—

**ARJUN**
Two hundred thousand people, one hundred and thirty-eight Guardians, and my sister was in the Anjari market buying thread.
(beat)
I've been writing *"you are not allowed to use the current"* in a book for twelve years, and I never once wrote down *why*, because some part of me *knew*, and it kept the page blank on purpose.

He stands. He is not crying. He puts the helmet back in the drum, closes it, and squares it on the rack, neat, like a man tidying his own grave.

**ARJUN (CONT'D)**
(to Kira, who is 17 and has never seen an adult break)
Don't learn anything from this.

**FLASHBACK — 2059, ANJARI MARKET, 14:12.** Four seconds, no sound: a girl of fifteen, long plait, school satchel, choosing thread at a stall. She looks up at a white light with mild curiosity.

**BACK TO BOOTH.**

The archive lights change from amber to red. A door seal cycles.

**OSE (V.O., over the PA, calm)**
Commander Sen. You have four officers in the corridor and one exit. Please don't make me revise my opinion of you.
