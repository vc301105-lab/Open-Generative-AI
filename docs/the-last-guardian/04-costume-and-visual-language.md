## PART A — COSTUME DESIGN

> **COSTUME LAW:** Each character has a numbered COSTUME STATE. A state may only change at the scene boundaries listed. Within a state, silhouette, fabric, colour, fastenings and accessories are identical in every frame. Dirt, wetness and damage accumulate forward only and never reset.

### ARJUN VEDH

**A1 — CADET (Scenes 1, 15 flashback, 13 flash) — 2059**
- Corps cadet undersuit: charcoal ribbed lattice-weave, high collar, exposed brass conduit ports at C7 and both forearms.
- Half-plate over chest and left shoulder only (cadets don't get full plate): brushed brass-bronze, hexagon with one open side stamped on the left pectoral.
- Utility belt, single thigh strap, soft boots.
- Bare head. No stubble. No grey streak. Clean, new, ironed — everything about him is *unearned* and it should read that way.
- **Damage progression within Sc. 1/15:** clean → soot on the right side → the left shoulder plate blistered and warped by the discharge. In Sc. 15's continuation, the warping happens on camera.

**A2 — SALVAGE DIVER (Scenes 2, 3) — present**
- Patched neoprene half-suit, faded black-to-brown, rolled to the waist above water.
- Canvas harness with brass D-rings; **regulator socket empty from Sc. 3** (Kira stole it).
- Rebreather backpack, corroded copper, single amber lamp on the mask (mask worn on the crown when not diving).
- Wrapped hands, cut-off gloves. Cord-and-brass anchor-stone at the right hip. Notebook pouch on the chest strap.
- Palette: rust, oxide, dull brass. He is the same colour as the water.

**A3 — CITY GREY (Scenes 4–10) — present**
- Over A2's base layer: a heavy oiled canvas coat, storm-grey gone green with age, collar up, elbow patched in a mismatched brown.
- Grey scarf that doubles as a face-wrap in the Nullpin sweep.
- **From Sc. 6:** a burn hole through the right sleeve and a split brow taped from Sc. 10.
- **From Sc. 9:** ash and blood on the left front panel that never comes out.

**A4 — FLATS (Scenes 11–19)**
- Coat abandoned (left on the Tidewall, Sc. 10). Down to the neoprene half-suit and a bleached long-sleeve undershirt scavenged at the flats — the only near-white garment he wears before the finale, so he reads as part of the salt.
- Red boot-lace now tying the notebook shut (planted Sc. 11).

**A5 — THE MANTLE (Scenes 23–29) — hero costume**
- Recovered Corps Bastion mantle from the Drowned Cathedral rack, rebuilt by Bhaskar and Meera:
  - **Undersuit:** charcoal lattice-weave, matte, with visible conduit channels tracing the wearer's actual Threadwork paths (so the suit's lines and his scars align — a design idea worth the whole costume).
  - **Chest:** a segmented brass-bronze cuirass, salt-pitted, three rivets replaced with mismatched steel ones (Bhaskar's repair).
  - **Shoulders:** an asymmetric left pauldron only — the right is bare weave, because a bastion always stands with the left forward.
  - **Mantle:** a short weather cloak, deep teal, torn hem, worn over the right shoulder and clipped at the left hip.
  - **Forearms:** open-channel bracers that let his Threadwork show; when he draws, cyan light runs from bracer to palm.
  - **Belt:** salvage-diver's harness, unchanged — he keeps his own history on his hips.
  - **No helmet. Ever.** The film's rule: we always see his face.
- **Damage in Act III:** scorched right side (Sc. 25), a resonance-blade slash across the cuirass (Sc. 26), and in Sc. 29 the entire suit's conduit channels blow out and go dead grey.

**A6 — AFTER (Scene 30)**
- A plain undyed cotton shirt, sleeves rolled, worn linen trousers, barefoot on the jetty. No harness, no stone, no notebook (Riya carries it).
- His Threadwork is still visible but is now **dull, dead pewter — no light, ever again.**

### KIRA OKONKWO

**K1 — KITE RUNNER (Scenes 3–10)**
- Cut-down men's work jacket, saffron faded almost to bone, sleeves hacked off at the shoulder.
- Teal vest, grey cloth forearm wraps (both arms, always), patched cargo shorts over leggings, split-toe rubber boots.
- Kite spool on a belt clip; three copper clips in her hair; one brass earring.
- Everything is a size too big and taken in with visible stitching she did herself.

**K2 — FLATS (Scenes 11–18)**
- Same base, plus a bleached grey wind-shirt tied at the waist and salt-crusted boots.
- **From Sc. 11:** Arjun's returned rebreather regulator hangs from her belt as a good-luck charm and stays there for the rest of the film — including in the Ark, which is how Dev knows she's been with Arjun.

**K3 — ARK CAPTIVE (Scenes 22, 27)**
- Hollow-issue: a plain bone-white shift and soft white trousers. No shoes. Hair released from the knot but **still six braids**.
- Her own copper clips and the brass earring have been removed and placed in a small white dish — visible in frame. She takes them back in Sc. 27, one at a time, while refusing her father. This is her costume arc's payoff.

**K4 — TETHER (Scenes 24–25, 28–29)**
- Recovered Corps Tether mantle, cut down two sizes by Meera:
  - Charcoal weave undersuit, **gold** conduit channels (vs Arjun's cyan).
  - No cuirass — tethers need the ribs free. Instead a segmented harness of brass rings across the chest and back, like a rigger's rig.
  - Bracers with six line-ports across the knuckles.
  - A short saffron half-cloak — the one colour she keeps from her old life, cut from her kite.
  - Boots re-soled with climbing rubber.

**K5 — GUARDIAN (Scene 30)**
- The same mantle, cleaned and properly fitted, saffron cloak now full-length, the open-sided hexagon newly stamped on her left bracer.
- The copper clips are back. The regulator is still on her belt.

**D2 — THE HOLLOW (Scenes 17, 22, 28, 29)**

### THE HOLLOW (rank and file)
Bone-white hooded lacquer coats over grey weave, faceless smooth half-masks covering nose to chin only (eyes always visible, always calm), soft-soled boots. They are silent and they walk. **No two Hollow may ever run in this film.**

### COASTAL AUTHORITY (rank and file)
Storm-grey riot weave, oxblood chevron on the left shoulder, full-face storm visors, Nullpin launchers. Deliberately over-equipped and clumsy in the rain — they slip, they struggle, they are not elite.

### STILTWAY CIVILIANS
Saffron, teal, oxblood, marigold, plastic sheeting as rain-cape, flip-flops, gold jewellery, work coveralls, school uniforms. **The Stiltway is the most colourful thing in the film and the Ark is the least. That is the whole design argument.**

## PART B — COLOUR & VISUAL LANGUAGE

### B.1 The three palettes

| | **THE DELTA (life)** | **THE FLATS (the past)** | **THE ARK (the cure)** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Base | Wet slate grey-green | Bone white, zero saturation | Bone white, zero saturation |
| Accents | Saffron, teal, oxblood, marigold, brass | None permitted except character power | None permitted except Amara's red bracelet |
| Light | Practical-heavy, motivated, messy, warm pockets | Enormous flat top-light, shadowless, cold | Even, sourceless, shadowless, clinical |
| Contrast | High, dirty blacks | Low, milky, lifted blacks | Low, crushed pure whites |
| Texture | Rain, steam, smoke, particulate | Salt dust, absolute stillness | Nothing. No atmos at all. |
| Feeling | Alive and struggling | Erased | Peaceful and wrong |

**The rule that makes it work:** the Flats and the Ark look almost identical. The audience should feel, on arriving at the Ark in Sc. 22, that they have been here before — because Dev is not offering a new world, he is offering the Anjari Salt Flats applied to every mind on Earth. Nobody says this line. The colour says it.

### B.2 Power colour signatures (LOCKED)
| Character | Colour | Behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| Arjun / Bastion | **Cyan-white #B9F2FF core, #2FB8D6 edge**, warm amber #FFAE5C when overstrained | Hexagonal tessellation, crystallises from an anchor point outward, audible *chime* |
| Kira / Tether | **Gold-white #FFE9A8 core, #E8A33D edge** | Linear, whipping, catenary sag under load, audible *sing* like a wet finger on glass |
| Dev / Loom | **Cold white-blue #DCEBFF, no warmth ever** | Architectural, appears fully formed (never builds), silent |
| Sera / Mirror | **No colour of her own** — she only ever shows other people's light back | Silvered, inverted, a half-frame delay |
| The Concord | **Pure #FFFFFF, blowing out to clipping** | Fills frame, removes shadow, removes colour, removes sound |

### B.3 Lens & format language
- **Format:** 2.39:1, anamorphic, shot on large-format digital with detuned vintage anamorphic glass. Blue horizontal flares are *permitted only from Cradle light sources* — never from lamps. This makes the flare a story element.
- **Act I:** 32 mm–50 mm handheld, close, shoulder-level, breathing. The camera is hiding with him.
- **Act II-A (Flats):** 25 mm–40 mm, locked off, tripod, wide, symmetrical. The camera stops hiding.
- **Act II-B:** longer glass, 75 mm–135 mm, compressed, shallow, isolating. Everyone is alone.
- **Act III:** 21 mm–40 mm handheld with a stabilised head — wide, close, chaotic, big.
- **The Concord/mind-space (Sc. 17, 22):** perfectly still, symmetrical, dead-centre framing, 40 mm, no camera movement at all, ever. Stillness = wrongness.
- **Scene 30:** 50 mm, handheld but calm, natural light only. The film exhales.

### B.4 Grain, texture and finish
- Fine 35 mm-style grain throughout, increasing by ~20% in flashbacks and Bleaching moments.
- The Flats and the Ark get *less* grain — smoother, cleaner, more digital. Cleanliness is the antagonist's texture.
- Sc. 19 (Arjun's worst Bleach) is the only sequence with a deliberate digital artefact language: micro-stutters, a dropped frame at a name, and a 4-frame repeat when he loses Riya.

### B.5 The one-clear-sky rule
Rain, cloud, ash, spray, smoke or salt-haze in **every** frame of the film until Scene 29's final beat. The first clean blue sky in 2 hours and 5 minutes should feel like oxygen.
