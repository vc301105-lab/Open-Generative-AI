# THE LAST GUARDIAN — CONTINUITY BIBLE
### Document 08 of 10 · Character continuity · Location continuity · Prop & wound clocks

> **THE CONTINUITY SUPERVISOR'S FIRST LAW**
> A generated shot that violates any locked attribute below is **rejected and re-rendered**. It is never colour-corrected, cropped, retimed or "lived with". Continuity in a film assembled from generated shots is not a department — it is the load-bearing wall of the whole illusion.

### B.5 The frame-match obligation (Scenes 1 ↔ 15)
Shots **1D/1E/1J** and **15B/15C/15D** are the same photography.
1. Shoot/generate Scene 1 first and lock the plates.
2. Scene 15's replay shots are the **identical plates** passed through the helmet-cam degrade pass (4:3 crop inside 2.39, −60% saturation, scan lines, exposure pumping, band-limited audio).
3. Only shot 15D **continues past** the Scene 1 cut point, and the continuation must match lighting, lens, blocking and wardrobe damage exactly at the splice.
4. **Any drift here destroys the midpoint and therefore the film.** This is the highest-priority continuity task in the production.
